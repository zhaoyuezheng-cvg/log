💡 点击每道题即可展开答案与解析。

答案：under

句意：Tom 坐在树下开始写日记。“在树下”用介词 under（在……下面）。

答案：explored

日记记录的是当天已经发生的事，全文为一般过去时（shone, began, wrote, discovered 等），故用 explore 的过去式 explored。

答案：branches

whose 引导定语从句修饰 an ancient tree，树的树枝不止一根，且谓语为 seemed（复数形式对应复数主语），故用复数 branches。

答案：taught

爷爷教他识别树龄是过去发生的事，用一般过去时，teach 的过去式为 taught（不规则变化）。

答案：filled

考查固定搭配 fill…with…（用……装满……），此处叙述过去的动作，用过去式 filled。

答案：fresh

修饰名词 flowers and seeds 需要用形容词，fresh 本身就是形容词，用原形即可（无比较级语境）。

答案：him

bring sb. sth.（给某人带来某物），动词后作宾语要用宾格，he 的宾格是 him。

答案：happily

修饰动词 wrote，要用副词，happy 的副词形式为 happily（变 y 为 i 加 -ly）。

答案：the

序数词 last 前要用定冠词 the，the last page 意为“最后一页”。

答案：but

考查固定搭配 not only…but also…（不仅……而且……），前后连接两个并列谓语。

In 1989, a huge earthquake hit a small town suddenly. After it happened, a father rushed to his son's school, only to discover that all the buildings fell down. The father remembered his promise to his son: “ No matter what happens, I'll always be there for you!” With tears in his eyes, he started to dig(挖) through the ruins(废墟).

As he was digging, other parents tried to stop him, saying, “It's too late. They're all gone.” The father asked, “Will you help me now?” But no one answered, so he kept digging alone.

Firefighters and police came, telling him it was dangerous, but he asked, “Will you help me now?” No one helped, so he dug alone.

He dug for 8 hours… 12 hours… 24 hours… 36 hours… Then, after 38 hours of digging, he pulled back a large stone and heard his son's voice.

He screamed his son's name, “Tom!”

He heard back, “Dad?! It's me, Dad! And 14 other kids here. I told them not to worry. I told them that if you were alive, you'd save me. And when you saved me, they'd be saved.”

“Come out, my boy!”

“No, Dad! Let the other kids out first, because I know you'll save me! No matter what happens, I know you'll always be there for me!”

参考答案：He rushed to his son's school.

细节题。定位第一段第二句 “After it happened, a father rushed to his son's school…”，at once 与 rushed 对应。注意用完整句子作答，时态与原文一致用一般过去时。

参考答案：He dug through the ruins alone with his hands for 38 hours.

细节归纳题。文中反复出现 “he started to dig through the ruins”“he kept digging alone”“he dug alone”“after 38 hours of digging”，概括成一句即可。答题要点：dig through the ruins / alone / 38 hours。

参考答案：After 38 hours of digging, when he pulled back a large stone.

细节题。定位第四段最后一句 “Then, after 38 hours of digging, he pulled back a large stone and heard his son's voice.” 用 when 引导的时间状语从句或 after 短语作答均可。

参考答案（言之成理即可）

① I learned that we should always keep our promise. （要信守承诺）
② I learned that we should never give up hope, no matter how hard it is. （永不放弃希望）
③ Love and trust between family members can work miracles. （爱与信任能创造奇迹）
④ The son thought of others first, and we should learn to be selfless. （先人后己、无私）
开放性题答题技巧：先亮观点（I learned that…），再用文中的情节作支撑（如父亲坚守承诺挖了 38 小时、儿子相信父亲一定会来救他），最后可加一句自己的感受。

易混辨析

① sound / voice / noise
sound 泛指各种“声音”；voice 常指人的“嗓音”（如 his son's voice）；noise 指“噪音”。
例：Light travels faster than sound. / She has a sweet voice. / Don't make so much noise.
② remember to do / remember doing
remember to do sth 记得去做某事（还没做）；remember doing sth 记得做过某事（已经做了）。
例：Remember to close the door when you leave. / I remember seeing him before.
③ hit / strike
都可表示自然灾害“袭击”。hit 的过去式仍为 hit；strike 的过去式为 struck。
例：A typhoon struck the coastal city. / A big earthquake hit the town.
