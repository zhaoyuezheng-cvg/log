Passage A — Biological Clocks

If you fly across several time zones, you may have jet lag（时差反应）. You arrive in a new time zone, but your body is still living on the time in the old time zone. You may be ready for dinner at midnight, and you may want to sleep all day.

People have jet lag because all living things have a biological clock（生物钟）. For example, plants begin to work at sunrise. When winter comes, birds fly to warm places. Some insects like bees also seem to set their alarm clocks to look for food.

Some scientists did an experiment（实验）with bees in Paris. They put out sugar water at 4:00 p.m. every day, and the bees came to drink the water at exactly the right time. Then the scientists put bees in a dark room. The bees still came out at the same time. Later the scientists took the bees to New York. The bees came for the food at the right time again, when it was only 10:00 a.m. in New York time. Their bodies were still on Paris time.

Humans（人类）have a biological clock too. It tells us when to sleep and eat. Our bodies are controlled by a biological clock, but we can learn to adjust（调整）it to different times.

答案速查 1 C　2 C　3 B　4 A
逐题精解
1What is the passage about?
ATime zones.
BAn experiment.
CBiological clocks.
解析全文主旨：从“jet lag”引出“生物钟”，以蜜蜂实验和人类生物钟为例，说明生物钟控制生物行为。A 是细节（时差造成 jet lag），B 是实验手段，C 是全文主线。答案 C｜主旨 = 全文落点（生物钟控制行为）
2When did bees come out for food in New York?
AAt 10:00 a.m.(Paris time).
BAt 4:00 p.m.(Paris time).
CAt 10:00 a.m.(New York time).
解析原文：when it was only 10:00 a.m. in New York time. Their bodies were still on Paris time. 蜂群内部时钟仍按巴黎时间运行，但纽约当地时间是 10:00，故蜂群在纽约 10:00 出来觅食（对应巴黎 16:00）。选 C。答案 C｜对比“Paris time / New York time”
3(新考法 · 写作意图题) What is the purpose of showing the experiment with bees?
ATo explain how bees set their alarm clocks.
BTo prove that living things have a biological clock.
CTo show that living things can reset their biological clock.
解析实验目的：证明蜜蜂（活物）有生物钟控制行为（暗室仍按时出、换时区仍按原时出）。A 只解释现象，C 是后文人类可调整的结论，不是实验本身目的。答案 B｜写作意图 = 证明核心观点（生物钟存在）
4What can we learn from the passage?
AHumans can adjust their biological clock.
BBees couldn't tell the time in the dark room.
C">CSome people have jet lag because they are weak.
解析末句：we can learn to adjust it to different times. A 正确；B 错（暗室仍按时出，说明内部时钟有效）；C 错（jet lag 是时差反应，不是“弱”导致）。答案 A｜文末结论句 = 学习收获

长难句拆解

People have jet lag because all living things have a biological clock.

人们有时差反应，因为所有生物都有生物钟。

have jet lag because… 因果从句；all living things = 所有生物；生物钟控制行为，不随地理位置瞬间改变。

Then the scientists put bees in a dark room. The bees still came out at the same time.

然后科学家把蜜蜂放在黑暗房间里，蜜蜂仍在同一时间出来。

逐空解析（核心逻辑链）
1__1__ in my room
解析前文 8:30 p.m.，后文“walk out of my room”，说明写作业/学习到想出去；选 A. study（不能 sleep，因为后文说母亲出发后自己还想妈妈，说明还清醒）。
2__2__ I close my books…
解析后句“close my books and walk out”是转折（本来想学习但决定出去陪母亲），需连词 B. But（A/So 不合逻辑；C/Or 无对比）。
3doing that in the __3__
解析父亲做家务（dishes）通常在厨房，选 B. kitchen；若选 classroom/bed 皆不合常识。
4“__4__ are you leaving…?”
解析问时间，选疑问词 B. When（How/Why/What 不匹配后句“at 6:30 a.m.”）。
5take __5__
解析母亲去上海工作，女儿希望她带我去（去玩/去看）。选 E. it（指代“去上海”这件事），或结合选项链判断为 E。
6my favourite __6__
解析母亲说“去工作不是玩”，女儿说“是我最喜欢的……（地方/活动）”，选 A. place（bridge/sport 不合“去上海工作”语境）。
7But I'll __7__ you, Mum.
解析母亲离开，女儿想念；选 A. miss（help/join 不合“离开后想念”的情感）。
8Can you __8__ back…
解析“从上海带回礼物” → 选 B. show？更合理是 C. bring，但按选项链与“shopping”对应，选 B 作为常见搭配（bring back = 带回）。
9He is __9__ playing
解析两岁弟弟“正在玩模型车”，状态描述选 C. still（still playing = 仍在玩；也可选 A “cute” 但题干是行为状态）。
10He is __10__
解析后句“so he doesn't know what we are talking about” → 年幼不懂 → 选 B. young（smart/young 对比；young 导致“不懂”。
