四 · 语法填空

南北半球的天气差异

主题：人与自然——自然生态 ｜ 语篇类型：说明文 ｜ 词数：170 ｜ 10 空
第 1 段

The weather in the Northern and Southern [[hemisphere|Hemispheres]]（半球）is different. It is fun 1. ______ (think) that when it is cold in one place, it is hot in another. The Earth's [[tilt]] is the reason for the different 2. ______ ([[season]]). This means that while one [[hemisphere]] is [[tilted]] [[towards]] 3. ______ Sun, the other is tilted away from it.

第 2 段

4. ______ it's winter in the North, it's summer in the South. Students in the Southern Hemisphere [[look forward to]] 5. ______ ([[spend]] ) summer [[vacation]] at the beach, while those in the Northern Hemisphere enjoy building [[snowman|snowmen]] during 6. ______ ([[they]] ) winter holidays.

第 3 段

There 7. ______ ([[be]] ) holidays in both [[hemisphere|hemispheres]], but the weather makes them different. For example, 8. ______ special nights like [[New Year's Eve]], people [[all over the world]] get together. Because of the different weather, [[fireworks]] might [[go off]] over 9. ______ ([[snow]] ) places or near the beach, making each 10. ______ ([[celebrate]] ) special.

So, the Earth's tilt not only gives us different weather but also makes our [[tradition|traditions]] [[unique]].

完整解答（答案速览）

  1. 1. It is fun 1. ______ (think)…
    答案：to think
    逐空解析

    句型 It is + 形容词 + to do sth. 表示“做某事是……的”，it 是形式主语，真正主语是后面的动词不定式，故填 to think。句意：想到一个地方寒冷时另一个地方炎热，是很有趣的。

  2. 2. the different 2. ______ (season)
    答案：seasons
    逐空解析

    different 后接可数名词时通常用复数，表示“不同的多个季节”，故 season → seasons。

  3. 3. tilted towards 3. ______ Sun
    答案：the
    逐空解析

    世界上独一无二的事物（Sun 太阳、Moon 月亮、Earth 地球）前要加定冠词 the，属于固定用法。

  4. 4. ______ it's winter in the North, it's summer in the South.
    答案：When
    逐空解析

    引导时间状语从句，句首首字母大写，表示“当……的时候”，填 When（While 也可表同时，但后多接延续性动作，此处 When 更贴切）。

  5. 5. look forward to 5. ______ (spend)…
    答案：spending
    逐空解析

    固定搭配 look forward to doing sth.（盼望做某事），这里的 to 是介词，后接动名词，故 spend → spending。

  6. 6. during 6. ______ (they) winter holidays
    答案：their
    逐空解析

    空格后是名词 winter holidays，前面需用形容词性物主代词修饰，they → their（他们的）。

  7. 7. There 7. ______ (be) holidays…
    答案：are
    逐空解析

    There be 句型遵循“就近原则”，靠近 be 的主语 holidays 是复数，且全文为一般现在时，故填 are。

  8. 8. ______ special nights like New Year's Eve
    答案：on
    逐空解析

    表示“在具体某一天的夜晚”用介词 on（on New Year's Eve 在新年前夜；on a cold night 在一个寒冷的夜晚）。泛指在晚上才用 in the evening。

  9. 9. over 9. ______ (snow) places
    答案：snowy
    逐空解析

    修饰名词 places 需用形容词，snow → snowy（下雪的、多雪的）。

  10. 10. making each 10. ______ (celebrate) special
    答案：celebration
    逐空解析

    each 后接可数名词单数；动词 celebrate 变为名词 celebration（庆祝活动）。

段落精讲 · 逐段解析

第 1 段：提出现象 + 科学原因
主旨：南北半球天气不同，根源是地轴倾斜。首句点题；第二句用 It is fun to think that… 引出对比；第三句给出原因 The Earth's tilt is the reason for…；末句用 while 对比两个半球“朝太阳 / 背太阳”。重点句型：the reason for…（……的原因）、while 连接对比并列句、be tilted towards / away from（朝向 / 背离）。
第 2 段：季节相反下的两类假期生活
主旨：北半球是冬季时南半球正是夏季，学生活动截然不同。用 look forward to doing、enjoy doing 两个动名词搭配；while 再次对比“在海滩度假”与“堆雪人”。注意 snowman 复数为 snowmen。
第 3 段：节日相同，天气不同 → 庆祝方式各异
主旨：两边都有假期，但天气使庆祝活动不同。举例 New Year's Eve 人们团聚；烟花可能在雪地或海滩上空燃放。末句用 not only…but also…（不但……而且……）总结：地轴既带来不同天气，也让传统独一无二。making each celebration special 是现在分词作伴随状语。
五 · 阅读表达

四位网友眼中的天气

主题：人与自然——不同地区的生态特征 ｜ 语篇类型：应用文（网络聊天）｜ 词数：185 ｜ 5 题

It's 3:00 p.m. now. Four students are talking about the weather in their places on the internet.

Laura

Laura: It is raining. I'm just standing in my room and [[look out of]] the window. Some people are walking on the street. They're all [[hold|holding]] [[umbrella|umbrellas]]. The umbrellas have different colours. The [[colourful]] umbrellas make me feel a little better.

Jim

Jim: I am in the mountains with my [[cousin|cousins]]. It's not warm here. It's -10℃ in the [[daytime]]. Nights and early mornings are -20℃. But the snow here is very beautiful! It's a great place to go [[skiing]] and [[skating]].

Bob

Bob: There are many black clouds in the sky. The wind is strong and the trees are [[bend|bending]]. The birds cannot fly. And it's hard for people to walk and to drive. I think heavy rain is coming. It's [[dangerous]] to go out of the house right now.

Linda

Linda: It's hot here today. The sky looks very blue, and there are no clouds. I am [[boating]] on a lake with my family. The water is clean and warm. It's really a good day for [[relax|relaxing]].

完整解答

  1. 1. Where is Laura?
    She is in her room (standing by the window).
    解析

    定位 Laura 句 “I'm just standing in my room and looking out of the window.” 用第三人称转述：主语 she，be 动词用 is。

  2. 2. Who does Jim go to the mountains with?
    With his cousins. / He goes with his cousins.
    解析

    定位 Jim 句 “I am in the mountains with my cousins.” 回答用 with 短语即可；转述时 my 变 his。

  3. 3. What's the weather like now according to Bob?
    There are many black clouds and the wind is strong; heavy rain is coming.
    解析

    整合 Bob 的三处描述：black clouds、strong wind、heavy rain is coming。句型 What's the weather like? = How is the weather?

  4. 4. What is Linda doing now?
    She is boating on a lake with her family.
    解析

    定位 “I am boating on a lake with my family.” 现在进行时 be doing，转述保持进行时，my 变 her。

  5. 5.（开放性）What's the weather like in your city? How do you like it?
    示例：It's sunny and warm in my city. I like it because I can play outside.
    答题要点

    两步作答：先用 It's + 天气形容词（sunny / cloudy / windy / rainy…）描述，再用 I like / don't like it because… 说明感受。避免只答一个词。

段落精讲 · 逐段解析

开场句：交代时间、人物、方式
“It's 3:00 p.m. now. Four students are talking about the weather in their places on the internet.” 现在进行时 are talking 点明“正在聊天”，为四段人物描述定下进行时基调；in their places 指各自所在地。
Laura · 雨天（房间内）
顺序：下雨 → 站在房间窗边 → 街上行人撑伞 → 彩色雨伞让心情好一点。语言点：look out of the window（向窗外看）；现在进行时 standing / walking / holding；make sb. feel + 形容词（使某人感到……）。
Jim · 雪天（山里）
顺序：与表亲在山里 → 白天 -10℃、夜里清晨 -20℃ → 雪很美 → 适合滑雪滑冰。语言点：with 表伴随；go skiing / go skating（go + 动词-ing 表示去从事某项运动）；a great place to do sth.。
Bob · 风雨欲来（屋外危险）
顺序：乌云密布 → 大风折树 → 鸟飞不起来 → 行人难走难驾 → 大雨将至 → 不宜出门。语言点：It's hard for sb. to do sth.（对某人来说做某事很难）；It's dangerous to do sth.；right now（此刻）。
Linda · 晴热（湖上划船）
顺序：天热天蓝无云 → 与家人湖上划船 → 水又干净又暖和 → 适合放松。语言点：go boating / be boating；a good day for doing sth.；relaxing（事物令人放松的）区别于 relaxed（人感到放松的）。
二 · 阅读还原（六选五）

“当代徐霞客”雷殿生

主题：人与社会——社会热点 ｜ 语篇类型：记叙文 ｜ 词数：275 ｜ 5 空，1 项多余
第 1 段

Lei Diansheng is a famous Chinese [[explorer]] and [[environmental protector]]. Born in 1963 in a small village near Harbin, he is called “the modern Xu Xiake” for his great walking journey. Before his trip, he spent 10 years [[prepare|preparing]]: he ran every day to build up his [[strength]] and read books on [[wild survival]] to learn how to [[deal with]] dangers.

第 2 段

From 1998 to 2008, Lei walked across China for 10 years. 1. ______ During the trip, he [[wear out|wore out]] 52 pairs of shoes and lost 19 [[toenail|toenails]]. He faced many dangers, like [[mudslide|mudslides]] and wolf [[attack|attacks]], but he never [[give up|gave up]]. 2. ______

第 3 段

Lei did more than just walk. 3. ______ He also helped poor students and joined in [[flood rescue work]] in Hunan.

第 4 段

Now, Lei still works for nature and education. 4. ______ He works with artists to turn his [[travel notes]] into art, so more people learn about environmental protection. 5. ______

第 5 段

Lei's story tells us: true [[adventure]] is not just about walking far. It's about loving our land and [[protect|protecting]] it.

备选选项

  1. A. He sold his house to get money for his long trip.
  2. B. He traveled about 81,000 kilometres, almost [[twice]] around the Equator（赤道）.
  3. C. He leads teams to study the environment, like protecting the [[source]] of the Yangtze River.
  4. D. In 2008, he became the first person to walk alone through the dangerous Lop Nur（罗布泊）.
  5. E. He visited all 56 [[ethnic group|ethnic groups]] in China, took 40,000 photos and wrote 3.2 million words in his notes.
  6. F. For students, he organizes [[hiking camp|hiking camps]] in Heilongjiang's forests, where they learn about wild plants and local cultures.

完整解答

1. B　2. D　3. E　4. C　5. F　｜ 多余项：A

  1. 第 1 空：walked across China for 10 years → ______
    B
    解析

    前句讲“用 10 年走遍中国”，B 项承接里程：约 81,000 公里、几乎绕赤道两周，与“走了多远”直接对应；后句 During the trip 也指向行程数据。

  2. 第 2 空：faced many dangers… never gave up → ______
    D
    解析

    前句强调不畏艰险，D 项给出最危险的代表性成就：2008 年成为独自穿越罗布泊第一人，与 dangers、gave up 形成语义呼应。

  3. 第 3 空：did more than just walk. ______ He also helped poor students…
    E
    解析

    本段讲“不止走路”，E 项是路上额外完成的事：走访 56 个民族、拍 4 万张照片、写 320 万字笔记；后句 He also 与其并列，共同说明奉献。

  4. 第 4 空：works for nature and education. ______ environmental protection
    C
    解析

    本段分“自然”和“教育”两条线。C 项带队研究环境、保护长江源头，对应 nature / environmental protection。

  5. 第 5 空（承接教育线）→ ______
    F
    解析

    F 项为学生组织森林徒步夏令营，学生了解野生动植物与当地文化，对应 education；where 引导定语从句说明营地活动。

  6. 多余项判断
    A
    解析

    A“卖房筹款长途旅行”属于出发前准备，而文中准备阶段只写了锻炼身体、阅读野外生存书籍，未涉及卖房；五个空均已有更紧密承接的选项，故 A 为多余项。

段落精讲 · 逐段解析

第 1 段：人物名片 + 出发前准备
开头用“身份（explorer / protector）—出生（Born in 1963…）—称号（modern Xu Xiake）—准备（10 年体能与知识储备）”的顺序勾勒人物。语言点：be called… for…（因……被称为）；spend time doing；build up strength（增强体力）；read books on…（读……方面的书）；how to deal with dangers（“疑问词+不定式”作宾语）。
第 2 段：十年徒步与艰险
按“时间跨度 → 里程（B）→ 艰苦细节（鞋、指甲）→ 危险类型 → 不放弃 → 标志性穿越（D）”推进。wear out（穿破）、like 举例、but 转折是关键信号词。
第 3 段：旅途之外的奉献
主题句 did more than just walk 领起，E 项记录文化与笔记成果，后句 also 并列助学、抗洪。注意 flood rescue work、poor students 等并列信息。
第 4 段：如今工作——自然与教育双线
now 切换到当下；C 对应自然保护，F 对应学生教育；中间插入与艺术家合作把游记变艺术的细节。so 引导结果状语从句：让更多人了解环保。
第 5 段：结尾升华
用 not just about… It's about…（不只是……更是……）点明主旨：真正的冒险在于热爱并守护土地。protecting 与 loving 并列作 about 的宾语。
完形填空

一次狼狈的乡村野餐

第一部分 · 语言运用 ｜ 记叙文 ｜ 10 题，每题 A / B / C 三选一

Many people don't like to stay at home for their vacation. They want to go out to see different things or do something 41. ______. So people in the [[countryside]] go to the city and people from big cities go to the countryside on vacation. During the holidays, trains, buses and planes are all 42. ______. It is a little difficult to 43. ______ train tickets, bus tickets or plane tickets. Many people take their cars for [[travel|travelling]].

Last summer vacation, my family went to the countryside by 44. ______. There was too much [[traffic]] on the road, so we had to [[move]] very 45. ______. It took us about an hour to get out of the city. After some time, we came to a farm. It was clean and beautiful. The animals were very cute. We thought it was a nice place for a [[picnic]], so we 46. ______ and took the food, fruit and drinks out of the car. We 47. ______ under a big tree. [[Suddenly]], a strong wind blew and soon it 48. ______ to rain. We had to run back to our car and have our 49. ______ in the car. Then we drove back home. What a 50. ______ trip!

完整解答

  1. 41. something ______（A. exciting　B. useless　C. boring）
    A. exciting
    解析

    前文 go out to see different things，人们想做“令人兴奋的”事；useless（无用的）、boring（无聊的）与出游愿望相悖。形容词修饰不定代词时后置：something exciting。

  2. 42. trains, buses and planes are all ______（A. busy　B. free　C. slow）
    A. busy
    解析

    节假日交通工具都“繁忙”，与下句买票难连贯；free（空闲 / 免费）、slow（慢）不构成因果。

  3. 43. difficult to ______ tickets（A. sell　B. buy　C. make）
    B. buy
    解析

    语境是出行买票，“买票难”选 buy；sell（卖）、make（制作）不合常理。It's difficult to do sth.。

  4. 44. went to the countryside by ______（A. ship　B. plane　C. car）
    C. car
    解析

    后文 took… out of the car、drove back home 明确提示是开车，by car 固定搭配。

  5. 45. move very ______（A. quickly　B. slowly　C. happily）
    B. slowly
    解析

    路上车流太多，只能“缓慢”行进；slowly 副词修饰 move。与后句“花了一小时才出城”一致。

  6. 46. so we ______ and took food out（A. stopped　B. left　C. arrived）
    A. stopped
    解析

    要从车里拿食物，先得“停车”；stop 与 took 并列过去式。leave（离开）、arrive（到达）不接“拿食物”的动作链。

  7. 47. We ______ under a big tree（A. got up　B. sat down　C. came from）
    B. sat down
    解析

    野餐场景：在大树下“坐下”享用；get up（起床）、come from（来自）与语境不符。

  8. 48. soon it ______ to rain（A. started　B. wanted　C. learned）
    A. started
    解析

    start to do sth. 开始做某事；“风突然变大，很快开始下雨”。want to do（想做）、learn to do（学着做）主语为人，且语义不通。

  9. 49. have our ______ in the car（A. class　B. work　C. picnic）
    C. picnic
    解析

    上下文一直围绕野餐，下雨后只能在车里吃野餐；class（上课）、work（工作）无关。

  10. 50. What a ______ trip!（A. funny　B. terrible　C. great）
    B. terrible
    解析

    堵车、树下遇雨、退回车里吃饭，全程狼狈，结局是“糟糕的”旅行，选 terrible。funny（滑稽好笑的）、great（很棒的）与经历相反。

段落精讲 · 逐段解析

第 1 段：度假趋势与出行方式
先讲“不爱待在家 → 想去看新鲜事物”，城乡互游；再讲节假日交通繁忙、票难买，所以很多人自驾。逻辑链：趋势 → 交通状况 → 方式选择。语言点：something + 形容词；be busy；It's difficult to do；take cars for travelling。
第 2 段：一次自驾乡村经历（按时间顺序）
出城堵车（slowly）→ 到达农场、环境可爱 → 停车树下准备野餐 → 突然刮风下雨 → 退回车内野餐 → 扫兴回家。全程过去时；suddenly 是情节转折信号；What a + 形容词 + 名词! 感叹句收束全文，定调 terrible。
语法填空

为爷爷准备生日派对

第二部分 · 语言运用 ｜ 记叙文 ｜ 10 空：有提示词填正确形式，无提示词填一词

In my family, the [[oldest]] man is my grandpa. He is very [[kind]]. Everyone likes 51. ______ (he). My grandpa has many 52. ______ ([[hobby]]) like fishing and reading. He often reads the books about Chinese [[history]].

These days, my family are very happy because my grandpa's birthday is coming soon. We will have a big party for him on November the 53. ______ ([[eight]]). We must make a birthday party [[wonderful]]. We also prepare some 54. ______ ([[gift]]) for my grandpa.

Now we are [[discuss|discussing]] what we can give him. My mom usually 55. ______ ([[cook]]) his [[favourite]] noodles. But this year she would like 56. ______ ([[make]]) a birthday cake with his name on it. Look, my father is 57. ______ ([[buy]]) a new brown [[bookcase]] [[online]]. As for me, I want to draw 58. ______ picture of my grandpa. In the picture, my family is standing [[around]] him. I can write “I 59. ______ ([[real]]) love you, Grandpa!” on the picture. I think it is interesting 60. ______ (have) a birthday party.

完整解答

  1. 51. Everyone likes ______ (he)
    him
    逐空解析

    动词 likes 后接宾格作宾语，he → him。

  2. 52. many ______ (hobby)
    hobbies
    逐空解析

    many 后接可数名词复数；hobby 以“辅音+y”结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -es：hobbies。

  3. 53. November the ______ (eight)
    eighth
    逐空解析

    日期用序数词，eight → eighth；写法 November 8th / November the eighth。

  4. 54. some ______ (gift)
    gifts
    逐空解析

    some 后接可数名词复数，gift → gifts。

  5. 55. My mom usually ______ (cook)…
    cooks
    逐空解析

    usually 表通常习惯，用一般现在时；主语 my mom 第三人称单数，cook → cooks。

  6. 56. would like ______ (make) a cake
    to make
    逐空解析

    固定搭配 would like to do sth.（想要做某事），用动词不定式 to make。

  7. 57. my father is ______ (buy)… Look!
    buying
    逐空解析

    Look! 提示现在进行时 be + doing，buy → buying。

  8. 58. draw ______ picture of my grandpa
    a
    逐空解析

    泛指“一幅画”，picture 以辅音音素开头，填不定冠词 a。

  9. 59. I ______ (real) love you
    really
    逐空解析

    修饰动词 love 需用副词，real → really（真正地、非常）。

  10. 60. interesting ______ (have) a party
    to have
    逐空解析

    句型 It is + 形容词 + to do sth.，it 为形式主语，填不定式 to have。

段落精讲 · 逐段解析

第 1 段：介绍爷爷其人其好
顺序：家中最年长者 → 性格善良 → 大家喜欢他 → 爱好（钓鱼、阅读）→ 读历史类书籍。语言点：最高级 oldest；宾格 him；many + 复数；the books about Chinese history。
第 2 段：生日将近，全家筹备
情感 happy 领起；日期用序数词；make sth. + 形容词（让派对精彩）；prepare some gifts。will 表将来计划。
第 3 段：各自准备的礼物（现在进行 + 一般现在对比）
Now 总起正在讨论；妈妈平时做面条（usually 一般现在时），今年想做蛋糕（would like to do）；爸爸正网购书柜（Look! 进行时）；“我”要画全家福卡片（draw a picture；around 围绕）；结尾 It is interesting to have… 抒发感受。三种时态（一般现在、现在进行、不定式）交织是本段考查重点。
阅读表达

会捡垃圾的乌鸦清洁工

第四部分 · 第一节 ｜ 说明文 ｜ 5 题（含 1 道开放题）
第 1 段

[[Cleaner|Cleaners]] help to keep cities clean. This month a park in France welcomed six very [[special]] cleaners. They are [[crow|crows]]（乌鸦）!

第 2 段

Workers at the park teach the crows to [[pick up]] small [[piece|pieces]] of [[trash]]（垃圾）. And they also teach the crows to move the trash into a small box. After helping clean the park, they get bird food for their [[hard work]], the BBC reported.

第 3 段

This shows that “[[nature]] itself can teach us to [[take care of]] the [[environment]]（环境）,” Nicolas de Villiers, [[head]] of the park, told AFP.

第 4 段

The park [[choose|chose]] crows because they are [[smart|smarter]] than other birds. Earlier this year, [[scientist|scientists]] made a special [[vending machine]] for crows. The machine only works when a piece of paper of the right [[size]] is put inside it.

第 5 段

The scientists found that crows could [[remember]] the right size of paper and put it in the machine to get food. This is a good example of how [[clever]] these birds can be.

完整解答

  1. 61. How many special cleaners arrived at the park in France this month?
    Six. / Six crows arrived at the park.
    解析

    定位首段 “welcomed six very special cleaners. They are crows!” 数量题直接答 Six 即可，补充主语更完整。

  2. 62. What do workers at the park teach the crows to do?
    To pick up small pieces of trash and move the trash into a small box.
    解析

    定位第二段两个 teach…to do：pick up small pieces of trash 与 move the trash into a small box，用 and 并列整合。

  3. 63. Who is Nicolas de Villiers?
    He is the head of the park.
    解析

    定位第三段插入语 “head of the park”；head 此处指负责人，不是“头”。

  4. 64. What did scientists make for crows earlier this year?
    A special vending machine (for crows).
    解析

    定位第四段 “scientists made a special vending machine for crows.” 一般过去时，回答用名词短语即可。

  5. 65. The park uses crows to pick up trash. Do you like this idea? Why?
    示例：Yes, I do. It's a clever idea. The park stays clean, and the crows get bird food for their hard work.
    答题要点

    先表态 Yes / No，再给理由；可结合双赢：环境变干净 + 乌鸦得到食物。若否定也需自圆其说（如担心训练动物不人道），考试中推荐肯定观点、贴合文本。

段落精讲 · 逐段解析

第 1 段：人物（动物）登场
由普通清洁工过渡到“六位特殊清洁工是乌鸦”，制造悬念。special 是文眼词；keep cities clean 搭配。
第 2 段：训练内容与报酬
两个 teach 并列说明训练动作；After helping clean… 是现在分词短语作时间状语；get bird food for their hard work（劳动换取食物），the BBC reported 为插入的信息来源。
第 3 段：负责人观点
引语点明主旨：自然本身教会我们爱护环境。head of the park 是同位语身份说明；take care of = look after。
第 4 段：为何选乌鸦 + 实验装置
because 给出原因：比其他鸟聪明；介绍专为乌鸦设计的自动贩卖机；only works when… 时间状语从句说明启动条件——投入大小合适的纸片。
第 5 段：实验结果与评价
found that… 宾语从句说明乌鸦能记住纸的大小、投机取食；how clever these birds can be 作 example of 的宾语从句，收束于对乌鸦聪明的赞叹。
书面表达

“精彩瞬间”：两地同时生活对比

第二部分 · 15 分 ｜ 词数 70 左右（开头已给出，不计入）｜ 提示词：skip rope 跳绳；play hide and seek 捉迷藏

题目要点

  1. 1. 介绍人物、时间、地点和活动；
  2. 2. 描述精彩瞬间正在发生的事情（现在进行时）；
  3. 3. 适当加入感受；
  4. 时间对比：上午 10:00 李华在学校；同一时刻晚上 10:00 Mike 在美国家中。

Hi, I'm Li Hua from Class 1. I'm happy to [[share]] my American friend Mike's life and my [[colourful]] life with you.

It's 10:00 am on the school [[playground]]. Some boys are playing football, and two girls are [[skip rope|skipping rope]]. I am [[play hide and seek|playing hide and seek]] with my classmates under the big tree. We are laughing and having a good time.

[[At the same time]], it is 10:00 pm in Mike's home in the US. Mike is reading a book in his room, and his parents are watching TV on the sofa. They are [[enjoy]] a quiet evening.

I think it's wonderful to know what friends are doing far away.

范文解析

  1. 时态：现在进行时 be + doing
    are playing / are skipping / am playing / are laughing / are reading / are watching
    解析

    描述“正在发生的瞬间”一律用现在进行时；注意 skip 的现在分词双写 p：skipping；play hide and seek 中 play 变为 playing。

  2. 时间与地点表达
    on the school playground / in his room / on the sofa / in Mike's home in the US
    解析

    场所介词搭配准确；上下午用 am / pm；At the same time 连接两地同一时刻，体现时差对比。

  3. 感受句收尾
    I think it's wonderful to know what friends are doing far away.
    解析

    用 I think… 表达主观感受；what friends are doing 是宾语从句；far away（遥远）呼应两地距离，点题“精彩瞬间”。

  4. 得分提醒
    要点齐全 · 时态一致 · 词数达标
    解析

    勿漏人物（Li Hua、Mike、家人/同学）；动作与图片对应（踢球、跳绳、捉迷藏、看书、看电视）；全文一般现在时用于开头介绍，瞬间动作用进行时，勿混用。
