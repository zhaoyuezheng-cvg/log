Jiliang on the CCTV Spring Festival Gala

On the night of 16 February, 2026, a wonderful song called Jiliang came out on the CCTV Spring Festival Gala. The famous singer Zhou Shen and the "Guizhou Village T" team sang it together.

This song is a creative work. It mixes traditional Chinese culture with modern music together. It takes the magical horse "Jiliang" from the ancient book Shan Hai Jing as its main idea, bringing this magical animal to life for people today.

The song uses traditional Chinese musical instruments and beautiful pictures. It connects the ancient myth of the sun tree with the bronze sacred tree（青铜神树） from Sanxingdui, creating a dialogue（对话） between Bashu culture and ancient Chinese civilization. The performance shows the Chinese nation moving forward, and gives traditional culture new life today. It is not only a great artwork, but also a way to show respect for our roots.

This song tells us that young Chinese musicians can create beautiful works by drawing from our rich culture. It encourages us to love our traditional culture and use it to create new art. In the future, more young people will surely use music to show the beauty of China and share it with the world.

参考译文 · 春晚歌曲《吉量》

2026 年 2 月 16 日晚，一首名为《吉量》的美妙歌曲登上了央视春节联欢晚会。著名歌手周深与"贵州村 T"团队共同演唱。

这首歌是一个创意之作，将中国传统文化与现代音乐融合在一起。它以古书《山海经》中的神马"吉量"为主旨，让这一神奇动物在今天的 people 面前鲜活起来。

歌曲使用了中国传统乐器和优美画面，把太阳树的古老神话与三星堆的青铜神树连接起来，在巴蜀文化与中华古代文明之间创造了一场对话。表演展现了中华民族奋勇向前，也赋予传统文化新的生命力。它不仅是伟大的艺术作品，更是表达对我们根脉的敬意的方式。

这首歌告诉我们，中国年轻音乐人可以从丰富的文化中汲取灵感，创作出美好作品。它鼓励我们热爱传统文化，并用它创造新的艺术。未来，更多年轻人定会用音乐展现中国之美，与世界分享。

My First Trip to Yangzhou

This is my first trip to China during the National Day holiday. It was a very wonderful experience. I got to the clean, lively city of Yangzhou with my family by plane in the morning. The hotel we stayed in was very comfortable and inexpensive.

Yangzhou, an ancient city, is in the eastern part of Jiangsu Province. There are lots of places of interest. At first, we visited the Slender West Lake（瘦西湖）, one of the most famous tourist attractions in China. It is such a beautiful lake that nobody should miss it! The Slender West Lake is as beautiful as the West Lake in Hangzhou, but its shape is long and narrow, so it has its name. Visiting the Slender West Lake is just like enjoying a Chinese painting. When we went boating, we saw many fish swimming and beautiful flowers in the sun. It just took my breath away. I couldn't help picking a flower, but my father said it was bad to hurt the flowers. Besides, the Slender West Lake is famous for the different kinds of fantastic ancient buildings. We walked along the Five-Pavilion Bridge（五亭桥） and enjoyed the ancient white tower next to it! The Five-Pavilion Bridge has a history of more than 200 years. The five pavilions are like five big lotus flowers on the lake. There are 15 holes in the bridge. A beautiful view can be enjoyed on full-moon nights as "15 moons" will be reflected on the lake. Also, there are other popular places for visitors to enjoy, such as the Twenty-Four-Bridge Scenic Area and the Ten Thousand Flowers Garden.

The Slender West Lake is not only famous for its beautiful views but also full of Chinese culture. Many writers, painters and artists in ancient times were interested in the sights and left many poems, paintings, handwriting and music. While enjoying the beauty of the Slender West Lake, visitors can also learn about interesting stories of Yangzhou.

参考译文 · 我的首次扬州之旅

这是我第一次在中国过国庆假期，是一次非常棒的经历。早上我和家人乘飞机抵达干净、充满活力的扬州。我们住的酒店既舒适又实惠。

扬州是一座古城，位于江苏省东部，有很多名胜古迹。我们先参观了瘦西湖——中国最著名的旅游景点之一。它是如此美丽，谁都不该错过！瘦西湖和杭州西湖一样美，但形状狭长，因此得名。游览瘦西湖就像在欣赏一幅中国画。我们划船时，看到许多鱼在游、阳光下鲜花盛开，美得令人屏息。我忍不住想摘花，但父亲说伤害花草不好。此外，瘦西湖以各式各样奇妙的古建筑闻名。我们沿着五亭桥散步，欣赏旁边的古白塔！五亭桥有 200 多年历史，五个亭子像湖上的五朵大莲花。桥有 15 个洞，月圆之夜，"十五个月亮"倒映湖中，美景尽收眼底。此外还有其他游客喜欢的地方，如二十四桥景区和万花园。

瘦西湖不仅以美景闻名，还充满中国文化。古时许多作家、画家和艺术家对这里感兴趣，留下许多诗篇、画作、书法和乐曲。游客在欣赏瘦西湖之美的同时，也能了解扬州的有趣故事。

阅读理解 C · 中澳假期差异Golden Week vs paid leave

主题 人与社会——文化差异词数 216难度 ★★★★☆建议用时 7′正确率 /4

Holidays in China and Australia

The first time I heard about China's "Golden Week"（黄金周） holiday, I was surprised. Everyone gets the same seven days off in one go! And I had never heard of "make-up days"（调休）. That's because holidays and leave are a bit different in my home country, Australia.

Australia has seven national-level public holidays. And each Australian state and territory（领地） also has a few special ones of their own added on. They celebrate local events.

Apart from national holidays, full-time and part-time workers in Australia get at least 20 days of paid leave a year. It doesn't matter how long they have worked. Also, you can get even more leave if you have worked in the same company for many years. In China, the most annual leave（年假） you can get is 15 days. It's given to those who have worked for over 20 years.

In Australia, many people like to take a few weeks of leave in one go. This way, you can get a nice, long holiday, especially around Christmas time and New Year's Eve. You can also "save" your leave. It will be added to your leave next year. This is the same for sick leave. Workers get 10 days of it a year.

参考译文 · 中国与澳大利亚的假期

我第一次听说中国的"黄金周"时很惊讶——所有人一次性都有同样的七天假！我从没听过"调休"。那是因为在我的祖国澳大利亚，假期和休假有点不同。

澳大利亚有七个国家级公共假日，每个州和领地还会额外增加几个自己的特殊假日，用来庆祝当地活动。

除国定假日外，澳大利亚的全职和兼职员工每年至少有 20 天带薪假，不管他们工作多久。如果在同一家公司工作多年，还能得到更多假期。在中国，最多能休 15 天年假，那是给工作 20 年以上的人的。

在澳大利亚，很多人喜欢一次休几周，这样就能拥有一个美好而漫长的假期，尤其是在圣诞和新年期间。你还可以"存"假——没休的假会加到下一年的假期里。病假也是这样，员工每年有 10 天病假。
