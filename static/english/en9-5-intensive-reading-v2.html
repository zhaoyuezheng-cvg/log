四、语法填空

阅读下面短文，在空白处填入一个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式。

主题：人与自我——优秀品行 | 语篇类型：记叙文 | 词数：167 | 难度：★★★☆☆

Peter 1. (look) at his new sports shoes now. They are the third pair he gets this term. The first pair is red and the 2. (two) pair is blue. Now he 3. (have) the black shoes. "Next time I want white ones," Peter thinks.

Peter's parents are busy. They 4. (not, have) much time to be with Peter. They feel sorry for that, 5. (so) they buy a lot of things he wants for him.

Peter sees his classmate Sam's pencil box. Sam's parents have good jobs. Why does Sam use 6. (an) old pencil box? Peter doesn't understand.

"Hey, Sam," Peter says. "Why not buy a new pencil box?"

"This one is still nice," Sam says. "And I 7. (save) money."

"For what?" Peter asks.

"I want to save 200 dollars this year 8. (help) some children in villages," Sam says.

Peter is surprised. Sam is doing a 9. (real) great thing. Then he starts thinking: Does he really need those new shoes, clothes and 10. (toy)?

答案与解析

1. is looking (提示词：look)

解析：句末有时间状语 now，表示正在进行的动作，主语 Peter 是第三人称单数，所以用现在进行时 is looking。

2. second (提示词：two)

解析：前文提到 first pair，此处指“第二双”，需用序数词 second。

3. has (提示词：have)

解析：表示现在的状态，主语 he 是第三人称单数，用 has。

4. don't have (提示词：not, have)

解析：描述一般情况，主语 They 是复数，否定句借助助动词 don't，后接动词原形 have。

5. so (无提示词)

解析：前句“父母感到抱歉”，后句“买很多东西”是结果，前后为因果关系，填 so（所以）。

6. an (无提示词)

解析：修饰单数可数名词 old pencil box，old 以元音音素开头，用不定冠词 an。

7. am saving (提示词：save)

解析：结合语境，Sam 正在攒钱，用现在进行时表示当前阶段正在进行的动作，主语 I，用 am saving。

8. to help (提示词：help)

解析：“攒钱”的目的是“帮助”孩子，动词不定式 to do 作目的状语。

9. really (提示词：real)

解析：修饰形容词 great，需用副词形式，real 的副词是 really。

10. toys (提示词：toy)

解析：与前面的 shoes, clothes 并列，表示泛指，用复数形式 toys。

五、阅读表达

阅读短文，根据短文内容回答下列问题。

主题：人与社会——文化遗产 | 语篇类型：记叙文 | 词数：183 | 难度：★★★☆☆

An old woman is wearing a cockscomb-shaped hat and black, red, yellow and green clothes and walking to people. She is showing Yi people's clothes to them.

The woman is Li Ruxiu. She is 63 years old. She lives in a small village in Yunnan. Yunnan is home to about 5,000,000 Yi people. Yi people are good at embroidery work. Like many Yi people, Li is good at making clothes. She learnt to do embroidery work at the age of eight and could make clothes at 12.

Li also likes collecting Yi people's clothes. In Li's house, there are over 7,000 pieces of Yi people's clothes. The clothes are very beautiful. There are nice patterns of flowers, birds, tigers and cats on them. Li gets some of the clothes from other people for free, and she buys some from the shops. She spends lots of money buying them, but she is happy to do that. She loves Yi people's clothes and

答案与解析

1. What is Li doing in beautiful clothes?

答案：She is showing Yi people's clothes to people.

解析：定位第一段第一、二句“...and walking to people. She is showing Yi people's clothes to them.” 直接提取答案。

2. Where does Li live?

答案：She lives in a small village in Yunnan.

解析：定位第二段第二句“She lives in a small village in Yunnan.” 直接提取。

3. What's Li good at?

答案：She is good at making clothes (and embroidery work).

解析：定位第二段“Like many Yi people, Li is good at making clothes.” 也可结合前文“good at embroidery work”，回答 making clothes 即可。

4. How many pieces of Yi people's clothes are there in Li's house?

答案：Over 7,000 pieces.

解析：定位第三段第二句“there are over 7,000 pieces of Yi people's clothes.” 直接获取数字信息。

5. (新考法·句子补全题)根据文章内容，续写句子。

答案：...she is happy to collect them / she wants to protect the culture. (言之有理即可)

解析：文章最后一句“She loves Yi people's clothes and...”不完整，结合前文她花大量钱购买衣服并且感到快乐（happy to do that），可以合理续写她喜欢收集这些衣服，或者她想要传承这种文化。

Test 1 一、阅读理解

阅读下面短文，从每题A、B、C三个选项中选出一个能回答所提问题或完成所给句子的最佳答案。

主题：人与自然——自然科学 | 语篇类型：说明文 | 词数：263 | 难度：★★★☆☆

① At 4:00 p.m., you are watching a football game live(现场直播) from Europe. At the same time, your friend in New York is sleeping—it's 4:00 a.m. there! Why is the time so different? Why do people in other places start their day when we are already in the middle of ours?

② That's because of time zones. Even though we live on the same planet(行星), people in different places are doing different things at the same time.

③ How do time zones work? The earth is like a big spinning(旋转的) ball. It turns from west to east. That's why the sun is coming up in the east first. The world is divided into(被分成) 24 time zones. Every time zone is one hour different from the next.

④ Let's look at some examples. In China, people in the East 8 time zone are getting up early and going to school or work. In London, people are enjoying afternoon tea at around 4 p.m. In New York, many people have just finished lunch at that time.

⑤ Time zones also bring some problems to our modern life. For example, a video(视频) meeting may start at 10 a.m. in Beijing, but it's midnight in New York. These time differences(时差) make it important to plan carefully and respect others' time.

⑥ Time zones don't just change the clock—they change our daily life. Time makes us different—but it also makes the world colorful and fun.

答案与解析

1. (新考法·写作手法题)How does the writer start Paragraph ①?

答案：A

解析：第一段通过描述一个具体的场景（下午4点你在看球赛，而纽约的朋友在睡觉），即“举例（giving an example）”的方式引出时差的话题，吸引读者兴趣。

2. (新考法·写作意图题)Why does the writer talk about a video meeting?

答案：B

解析：定位第五段第一句“Time zones also bring some problems to our modern life.”（时区也给我们的现代生活带来了一些问题），紧接着用视频会议举例说明时差带来的不便，因此是为了说明时区可能是个问题（To show time zones can be a problem）。

3. (新考法·图文匹配题)Which paragraph does this following picture come from?

答案：B

解析：图片展示了地球（The earth）、东西方向（West, East）以及太阳（The sun）的光线。这与第三段内容“The earth is like a big spinning ball. It turns from west to east. That's why the sun is coming up in the east first.”（地球像一个大旋转球，自西向东转，这就是为什么太阳先从东方升起）完全吻合。故选B。
