"Most studies show that many animals can feel weather changes." —— 动物是大自然最灵验的天气预报员。
Passage A

Animals and Weather

应用文 · 说明对象：动物行为与天气变化的对应关系。结构为"总—分—总"，表格承载核心信息，是细节题与出处题的重点。

主题 · 人与自然 — 自然环境 语篇类型 · 应用文 词数 · 163 难度 ★★★★☆ 建议用时 5′ 正确率 __ / 5

Most studies show that many animals can feel weather changes. Here are some interesting things about animals.

AnimalsBehaviours（行为方式）Weather changes
Birds A lot of birds get together and stay under the roof（屋檐）of houses. Heavy rain will come.
Sharks Sharks swim to deeper water. A big storm will arrive.
Spiders There are more spiders than usual in autumn, and the spiders are making larger webs（网）than usual. A cold and dry winter will come.
Spiders There are fewer spiders and they make smaller webs. A warm and wet winter will come.
Ants A lot of ants are on the grass. It is going to rain.

The world of animals is really amazing. Sometimes you can not explain（解释）why they can tell the weather before the weather report. If you want to know more about them, you can read more articles about science in School Science Magazine.

1If the spiders are getting more and they are making larger webs than usual in autumn, the weather in the following winter will be ______.

解析 定位表格蜘蛛（Spiders）第一种情形：more spiders than usual / larger webs than usual 对应的结果是 "A cold and dry winter will come."，即 dry and cold。注意选项把原文的 cold and dry 调换语序，故 B 正确。A（warm and wet）对应"蜘蛛更少、网更小"的情形；C 把冷热特征混搭，与原文不符。

2What can we learn from the passage?

解析 A 错：原文只说蜘蛛"数量更少、网更小"，并未说"冬天没有蜘蛛结网"，属于过度推断。B 错：鸟是"聚集在屋檐下"预示大雨，蚂蚁"出现在草地上"预示下雨，选项把两个不同条件混为一谈，偷换概念。C 对：末段 "Sometimes you can not explain why they can tell the weather before the weather report." 与选项意思一致，注意情态动词 can't 的温和表述正是推断题的正确项特征。

3（新考法 · 文章出处题）Where can we read the passage?

解析 末句给出明确出处线索：you can read more articles about science in School Science Magazine，且全文为科普类应用文，故选 C。storybook 需有人物与情节，travel guide 需有景点、路线等信息，均无依据。技巧：出处题先找杂志/栏目名称，再看文体特征。

逐段详细解析

第 1 段 · 总起
"Most studies show that many animals can feel weather changes." 用 Most studies show that…（大多数研究表明……）开门见山点明说明对象：许多动物能感知天气变化。"Here are some interesting things about animals." 起承上启下作用，引出下文表格。结构上属于"总—分—总"中的第一个"总"。
表格 · 分说
四种动物的行为与天气征兆一一对应：鸟聚集、躲在屋檐下 → 大雨将至；鲨鱼游向更深水域（deeper water）→ 大风暴来临；蜘蛛用两种相反情形作对比说明——数量更多、网更大 → 干冷的冬天，数量更少、网更小 → 温暖湿润的冬天；蚂蚁大量出现在草地上 → 天要下雨。注意 more／fewer、larger／smaller 两组反义比较级构成对照，是表格信息题的常考设错点。
末段 · 总结
"The world of animals is really amazing." 总括全文并抒发赞叹；"Sometimes you can not explain why they can tell the weather before the weather report." 说明人们有时无法解释动物为何能先于天气预报预知天气（对应第 2 题 C）；末句给出延伸阅读的出处 School Science Magazine（对应第 3 题）。结构上是第二个"总"，兼有拓展延伸功能。
积累小卷 · 集有所用 / 识有所悟 ① 比较级归纳：deeper—deep、more—many、larger—large、fewer—few、smaller—small；fewer 修饰可数名词复数，less 修饰不可数名词。
② be going to 表示根据现有迹象作出的主观判断（It is going to rain. 天要下雨了），区别于 will 表纯粹将来。
③ 读有所思（开放题）：如蜻蜓低飞（dragonflies fly low）、蚂蚁搬家、燕子低飞都预示将下雨。
④ 识有所悟·仿生学：蜘蛛丝 → 防弹衣、医用缝合线；鲨鱼皮肤 → 减阻高速泳衣与船体设计；蝙蝠回声定位 → 雷达与声呐。
Passage B

Zhang Dew and Winter Swimming

记叙文 · 按"背景—人物—评价"展开，用他人怕冷与主人公兴奋的对比引出人物，结尾以直接引语点明健身观。

主题 · 人与社会 — 体育锻炼 语篇类型 · 记叙文 词数 · 143 难度 ★★★★☆ 建议用时 5′ 正确率 __ / 4

It's December and it is about −10°C in Harbin these days. Many people think it's too cold, but Zhang Dew is excited about the cold days.

Zhang Dew lives in Harbin, Heilongjiang. He is seventy-four years old, but he looks young. Zhang loves winter swimming very much. It's snowy today and it is very cold. But look at the picture! Zhang is swimming in the Songhua River and he is having fun! Before he gets into the cold river, Zhang usually does some exercise like running to make himself warm. The cold and strong winds on the river can't make him afraid.

"Winter swimming helps me to be healthy and I never catch a cold. It makes me happy and I want to keep doing it. But this exercise isn't right for everyone. People with some health problems can't do it", Zhang says.

1The weather is ______ in Harbin in December.

解析 首句直接给出信息：It's December and it is about −10°C … Many people think it's too cold.，−10°C 与 "too cold" 都指向 cold，故选 B。warm、rainy 文中均无依据。

2What does Zhang usually do before getting into the river?

解析 细节定位句："Before he gets into the cold river, Zhang usually does some exercise like running to make himself warm."，running 与选项 C 的 "does some running" 同义复现，故选 C。A、B 均未提及。

3The underlined word "it" in the last paragraph refers to ______.

解析 代词指代题：末段 "It makes me happy and I want to keep doing it." 中的 it 回指上文反复出现的 winter swimming；后句 "But this exercise isn't right for everyone" 用 this exercise 同义复现，进一步印证。故选 B。技巧：代词指代题向前就近寻找同一名词或同义复现。

4What can we learn from the passage?

解析 C 与原句 "But this exercise isn't right for everyone." 完全一致，正确。A 与原文相反——下雪天他正在江里游泳且 having fun；B"擅长所有运动"属无中生有，原文只提到跑步与冬泳。

逐段详细解析

第 1 段 · 背景
交代时间（December）、地点与气温（Harbin，约 −10°C）；"Many people think it's too cold, but Zhang Dew is excited about the cold days." 用 but 转折形成"众人怕冷 vs 主人公兴奋"的对比，制造悬念，自然引出人物。
第 2 段 · 人物
先总写身份：家住黑龙江哈尔滨，74 岁却看起来年轻（looks young）；再写爱好：非常喜欢冬泳；接着用 "But look at the picture!" 把读者带入现场——今天下雪、天气很冷，他却在松花江里游泳而且很开心；随后补充细节：下水前通常做跑步等运动使自己暖和（to make himself warm 表目的）；江上的寒冷与强风不能使他害怕（can't make him afraid，make + 宾语 + 形容词）。
第 3 段 · 评价
直接引语写冬泳的益处：使人健康（helps me to be healthy）、从不感冒（never catch a cold）、心情愉快并想坚持下去（keep doing it）；末句以 But 转折点明理性态度——这项运动并非人人适合，有健康问题的人不能做，既是第 4 题依据，也体现科学健身观。
积累小卷 · 短语互译与完成句子 1. 冬泳 → winter swimming　2. do some exercise → 做运动，锻炼（exercise 表"锻炼"时不可数）
3. 感冒 → catch / have a cold　4. health problems → 健康问题
5. The exciting news made him excited.（-ing 修饰事物"令人…的"，-ed 修饰人"感到…的"）
6. just right for him（be right for = be suitable for，适合……）
Test 1 · B

The Saxophone — No Pain, No Gain

记叙文 · 按时间顺序写"梦想—尝试—练习—圆梦"，情感线索呈"向往 → 低落 → 回升 → 最高兴"，是情感态度题的典型素材。

主题 · 人与自我 — 自我提升 语篇类型 · 记叙文 词数 · 212 难度 ★★★☆☆ 建议用时 6′ 正确率 __ / 4

Five years ago, on my way home on the bus every night, I always found an old man playing his saxophone（萨克斯）near the river. I could see the big smile on his face. The beautiful music made me dream（梦想着）to play the saxophone just like that man.

Two weeks later, it was my birthday. I opened my present and found that it was a saxophone! I put it to my mouth to play, but it wouldn't make a sound. I blew（吹）and blew but I couldn't get the saxophone to make a sound. I was about to cry when suddenly the saxophone made a sound. I blew again, I moved my fingers on the saxophone and I made more sounds. Some of them were loud（大声的）and clear but many of them weren't. I was happy because I was playing very interesting sounds. But it wasn't beautiful music.

Then came the lesson. Night after night, week after week, I practised hard playing the saxophone. Slowly I started to get better and at last there came the magic day when the beautiful music came out of my saxophone.

Now I'm in the TV studio（电视台）with my saxophone. I'm so happy just like the old man near the river.

1What made the writer want to play the saxophone?

解析 定位首段末句："The beautiful music made me dream to play the saxophone just like that man."，梦想源于美妙的音乐，故选 B。A（笑容）只是"我"看到的表象；C（生日礼物）是实现梦想的契机，而非动机，属因果倒置。

2Why is the writer happy in the last paragraph?

解析 经过 "Night after night, week after week, I practised hard"，终于 "the beautiful music came out of my saxophone"，末段的高兴正源于练习后的成功，故选 B。A 未提及与老人重逢；C 把 magic 误接到 saxophone 上（原文 magic 修饰 day），属偷换概念。

3（新考法 · 情感态度题）Which of the following shows the writer's feelings?

解析 情感线索：五年前被音乐打动、心生向往（中等偏上，并非低落）→ 两周后拿到萨克斯却吹不出声，"I was about to cry"（短暂低谷）→ 发出有趣声音后"was happy"回升 → 刻苦练习不断进步 → 现在在电视台演出"I'm so happy"（最高点）。因此曲线应有起伏、整体上扬，选 B。A 的一路直线上升忽略了"几乎要哭"的挫折点，且与开头已有的喜悦不符；C 持续下降与结局完全相反。

4What lesson does the writer learn?

解析 全文围绕"反复刻苦练习 → 最终成功"展开（Then came the lesson… practised hard… get better），对应谚语 No pain, no gain.（一分耕耘，一分收获），故选 A。B "It's never too old to learn（活到老学到老）"强调年龄，文中未提；C "Learning without thinking is of no use（学而不思则罔）"强调思考，与主题无关。

逐段详细解析

第 1 段 · 起因
"Five years ago, on my way home on the bus every night" 交代时间与场景；"I always found an old man playing his saxophone near the river." 引出关键人物与事物；"I could see the big smile on his face." 写老人的快乐；末句 "The beautiful music made me dream to play the saxophone just like that man." 点明梦想的由来，注意 make sb. do sth.（使某人做某事）结构。
第 2 段 · 发展
"Two weeks later, it was my birthday." 时间推进，礼物正是一把萨克斯（present = gift）；接着写挫折——吹不出声音（wouldn't make a sound），"blew and blew but I couldn't…" 反复尝试仍失败；"I was about to cry when suddenly the saxophone made a sound." 用 be about to do … when …（正要做……这时……）写出情绪低谷与转折；随后继续吹、动手指，发出更多声音，"Some of them were loud and clear but many of them weren't."；末两句说明此时虽有小进步，但尚未成功，情感"先降后升"。
第 3 段 · 转折
"Then came the lesson." 短句过渡，引出练习阶段；"Night after night, week after week, I practised hard playing the saxophone." 用反复句式强调坚持与刻苦；"Slowly I started to get better" 写渐进式进步；"at last there came the magic day when the beautiful music came out of my saxophone." 写终于成功，呼应第 1 段的梦想。
第 4 段 · 结果
"Now I'm in the TV studio with my saxophone." 与开头"河边的老人"形成首尾呼应；"I'm so happy just like the old man near the river." 点明"我"也因音乐而快乐，情感达到最高点，主题升华：努力让梦想成真，也把快乐带给别人。

高频考点与方法归纳

细节理解题
先读题干、圈出定位词（数字、地名、专有名词），再回原文找同义复现（running → does some running）；选项常通过调换语序（cold and dry ↔ dry and cold）设置障碍。
推断题
警惕三类干扰项：过度推断（no spiders making webs）、偷换概念（把两个条件混为一谈）、绝对化表述（all / never / no）。正确项常含 can't、sometimes、often 等温和词。
代词指代题
向前就近回指，找同一名词或同义复现（it → winter swimming，由 "this exercise" 印证）；代入验证句意通顺。
出处题 / 情感态度题
出处题看文体 + 栏目/杂志名；情感态度题画出情感曲线，注意转折词（but、when、at last）带来的起伏，不能只看整体趋势。
语法聚焦
① 比较级：deeper—deep、more—many、larger—large、fewer—few、smaller—small（fewer 修饰可数复数，less 修饰不可数）。② be going to 表根据迹象的主观判断。③ make + 宾语 + 形容词 / 动词原形：make himself warm、make him excited、made me dream。④ -ing / -ed 形容词：exciting news（事物）／excited people（人）。⑤ keep doing sth.、be about to do … when …。
