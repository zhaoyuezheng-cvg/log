七选五 · 塑料污染 阅读短文，从方框内所给选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项（有一项多余）。

2025 · 江西 · 中考真题 主题：人与自然——环境保护 体裁：说明文 词数：约 180 难度 ★★★☆☆ 答案见下

Plastic pollution is a serious problem, and it’s getting worse. Plastic can stay for a long time. Scientists are trying to create plastic that breaks down quickly, but the best way for now is to use less plastic. 1E

Stop using plastic bags.

Instead, take your own reusable shopping bag to the store. Every year, people use tons of plastic bags worldwide. Some countries try to stop using plastic bags. 2F

Say no to the straw（吸管）.

A study showed that around 8.3 billion plastic straws cover the world’s beaches. So when you order a drink, say no to the straw, or bring your own reusable one.

3B

Carry a reusable water bottle with you. Around the world, nearly a million plastic bottles are thrown away every minute. The problem is so bad that in some countries, stores aren’t allowed to sell bottled water any more.

Avoid plastic packaging（包装）.

4G It’s almost everywhere. But there is something you can do, too: avoid fruit or vegetables packed in plastic.

Recycle what you can.

You can’t recycle all plastic things. 5D For example, you can recycle bottles and milk or juice bags. In some countries, machines take the bottles and give a little money back for each bottle.

A. They are fresh.
B. Don’t buy bottled water.
C. Bottled water is expensive.
D. But it is possible to start small.
E. Here are five tips that can help you.
F. Other countries make customers pay for them.
G. Most things you buy come in plastic packaging.

塑料污染是个严重的问题，而且越来越糟。塑料能存在很长时间。科学家们正试图造出能快速降解的塑料，但目前最好的办法是少用塑料。（1）这里有五条建议能帮到你。

停止使用塑料袋。

相反，去商店带上你自己的可重复使用的购物袋。全世界每年用掉成吨的塑料袋。一些国家试图停止使用塑料袋。（2）另一些国家则让顾客为塑料袋付费。

对吸管说“不”。

一项研究显示，全球海滩上覆盖着约 83 亿根塑料吸管。所以当你点饮料时，拒绝吸管，或者自带一根可重复使用的。

（3）别买瓶装水。

随身携带一个可重复使用的水瓶。在全世界，每分钟有近一百万个塑料瓶被扔掉。问题严重到在一些国家，商店不再被允许出售瓶装水。

避免塑料包装。

（4）你买的大多数东西都是塑料包装的。塑料包装几乎无处不在。但也有一件你能做的事：避免买塑料包装的水果或蔬菜。

能回收的就回收。

你无法回收所有塑料制品。（5）但从小处做起是可行的。例如，你可以回收瓶子、牛奶盒或果汁袋。在一些国家，机器回收瓶子并返还少量的钱。

（注：多余选项为 A「They are fresh.」与 C「Bottled water is expensive.」，文中无对应语境。）

阅读表达 · Jeff 的中文学习之旅 阅读短文，根据短文内容回答问题或翻译画线部分。

2025 · 湖南 · 中考真题 主题：人与自我——学习经历 体裁：记叙文 词数：约 200 难度 ★★★☆☆ 答案见下

My name is Jeff. I like learning foreign languages. When I retired（退休） in 2018, I could speak three foreign languages. At that time, my biggest wish was to learn a new one.

Later, I watched a program about Chinese culture on TV. I was so interested in it that I made the decision to learn Chinese. I found a cultural center in the north of London by chance. It offered Chinese language courses fit for me. In March 2019, I began to learn. To attend the classes, I left my home at 5 a.m. every Saturday and drove 3 hours to the cultural center. But this didn’t stop me. After class, I practiced a lot. Through learning, I developed a love for Chinese poems and joined in activities like Chinese poetry readings. I increasingly dreamed of visiting China.

My Chinese teacher Mr. Liu always mentioned “It’s better to travel ten thousand miles than read ten thousand books”. He suggested that I should take part in a study tour in China. So in August 2024, I visited Xi’an with my classmates of the cultural center. During the trip, as the student best at Chinese among us, I tried to act as an interpreter（口译员）. I helped my classmates in many ways like asking the way and shopping in Chinese. This experience encouraged me to study further.

The British often say “Learning is a lifelong pursuit（追求）”, which means “It’s never too late to learn”. I will keep on learning Chinese, hoping to be a lifelong learner. I believe that learning Chinese will bring even more possibilities to my life.

我叫杰夫。我喜欢学习外语。2018 年退休时，我会说三门外语。那时，我最大的愿望就是再学一门新语言。

后来，我在电视上看了一个关于中国文化的节目。我对此非常感兴趣，于是决定学习中文。我偶然在伦敦北部找到一个文化中心，它提供适合我的中文课程。2019 年 3 月，我开始学习。为了上课，我每周六早上 5 点离开家，开 3 个小时的车到文化中心。但这并没有阻止我。课后我大量练习。通过学习，我爱上了中文诗，还参加了中文诗歌朗诵等活动。我越来越梦想去中国看看。

我的中文老师刘老师总是提到“读万卷书不如行万里路”。他建议我参加中国的研学旅行。于是 2024 年 8 月，我和文化中心的同学一起去了西安。旅途中，作为我们中中文最好的学生，我试着担任口译员。我用问路、中文购物等方式帮助同学。这段经历鼓励我继续深造。

英国人常说“学习是一生的追求”，意思是“活到老学到老”。我会继续学习中文，希望成为一个终身学习者。我相信学中文会给我的人生带来更多可能。

Everything was new to me when I first arrived 2in China. As for seats at the table, Chinese people have their own culture. Once, I 3was invited (invite) to a big dinner at a restaurant. Before the dinner, someone showed me to take the seat 4that / which was ready for the most important person. It faced the door.

I found another 5interesting (interest) thing. Chinese people like giving gifts in pairs. Last month, I 6had (have) a party with three friends in my home. They each brought two gifts. I couldn’t wait to open them, 7but one of my friends reminded me not to do that right away. He thought it was 8impolite (polite) to open gifts in front of the givers.

These are amazing 9days (day) of cultural experiences. I believe my stay in China will not be 10the last time. I expect to come to China again.

中国是一个礼仪之邦。我在这里当外籍教师，很快就要离开了。我对中国文化和社会有了深入的了解。

初到中国时，一切对我来说都很新鲜。说到餐桌上的座位，中国人有自己的文化。有一次，我被邀请去一家餐厅参加一场盛大晚宴。开席前，有人指引我坐到为最重要的人准备的座位上，那个座位正对着门。

我发现了另一件有趣的事。中国人喜欢成双成对地送礼。上个月，我和三个朋友在家里聚会，他们每人带了两件礼物。我迫不及待想打开，但我的一个朋友提醒我别马上这么做。他认为当着送礼人的面拆礼物是不礼貌的。

这些是奇妙的文化体验时光。我相信这次中国之行不会是最后一次。我期待再次来到中国。
