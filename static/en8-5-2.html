Passage 1 · Say No to Plastic

七选五2025·江西·中考真题 ／ 阅读还原（5 空 + 7 选项）

Plastic is a serious problem, and it's getting worse. Plastic can stay for a long time. Scientists are trying to create plastic that quickly, but the best way for now is to use less plastic. ___1___

Stop using plastic bags. , take your own shopping bag to the store. Every year, people use tons of plastic bags . Some countries try to stop using plastic bags. ___2___

Say no to the (吸管). A study showed that around 8.3 plastic straws cover the world's beaches. So when you order a drink, say no to the straw, or bring your own reusable one.

___3___ Carry a reusable water bottle with you. Around the world, nearly a million plastic bottles are every minute. The problem is so bad that in some countries, stores sell any more.

Avoid plastic (包装). ___4___ It's almost everywhere. But there is something you can do, too: avoid fruit or vegetables packed in plastic.

what you can. You can't recycle all plastic things. ___5___ For example, you can recycle bottles and milk or juice bags. In some countries, machines take the bottles and give a little money back for each bottle.

选项 Options

A. They are fresh.
B. Don't buy bottled water.
C. Bottled water is expensive.
D. But it is possible to start small.
E. Here are five that can help you.
F. Other countries make customers pay for them.
G. Most things you buy come in plastic packaging.
✅ 答案与逐段解析（点击可收起）
1. E2. F3. B 4. G5. D 干扰项：A、C

逐段大意与解题线索

第 1 段｜塑料污染日益严重，塑料难以降解，目前最好的办法是少用塑料。
空 1 → E　下文五段各给一条建议，E 项「Here are five tips that can help you.」承上启下、总领全文。
线索：下文 5 个祈使句段落 = five tips
第 2 段｜建议一：拒绝塑料袋，自带可重复使用的购物袋；全球每年用掉大量塑料袋。
空 2 → F　「Some countries ... Other countries ...」并列对比，them 指代 plastic bags。
线索：Some … Other … 结构呼应
第 3 段｜建议二：拒绝吸管。全球海滩约有 83 亿根塑料吸管；点饮料时拒绝吸管或自带环保吸管。
第 4 段｜建议三：拒绝瓶装水。全球每分钟近百万个塑料瓶被丢弃，部分国家禁售瓶装水。
空 3 → B　段首主题句，后文「自带水壶」与「Don't buy bottled water」语义一致。
线索：bottled water / reusable water bottle 同话题复现
第 5 段｜建议四：拒绝塑料包装。塑料包装无处不在，可以少买预包装果蔬。
空 4 → G　G 项承接「plastic packaging」话题，并为下句 It's almost everywhere 中的 It 提供指代。
线索：代词 It 指代 plastic packaging
第 6 段｜建议五：能回收的尽量回收。可回收瓶子、牛奶袋、果汁袋，回收机还会返钱。
空 5 → D　「不能全部回收」与「But it is possible to start small」构成转折，后文 For example 正是「从小处做起」的例子。
线索：But 转折 + For example 举例印证

重点句型与语法

Instead, take your own reusable shopping bag to the store.
instead 作副词「作为替代」，常置句首/句末；本句为祈使句。
So when you order a drink, say no to the straw, or bring your own reusable one.
say no to sth. 拒绝某物；or「否则」；one = a reusable straw，避免重复。
A study showed that around 8.3 billion plastic straws cover the world's beaches.
that 引导宾语从句；billion 前有具体数字时不用复数（8.3 billion）。
The problem is so bad that stores aren't allowed to sell bottled water any more.
so + adj. + that 如此……以至于……；be allowed to do 被允许做（被动语态）；not … any more 不再。
But there is something you can do, too: avoid fruit or vegetables packed in plastic.
something (that) you can do 为定语从句（省略 that）；packed in plastic 过去分词短语作后置定语。

Passage 2 · Jeff's Chinese Dream

阅读简答2025·湖南·中考真题 ／ 回答问题 + 翻译画线句

My name is Jeff. I like learning . When I (退休) in 2018, I could speak three foreign languages. At that time, my biggest wish was to learn a new one.

Later, I watched a program about Chinese culture on TV. I was so interested in it that I to learn Chinese. I found a center in the north of London . It offered Chinese language me. In March 2019, I began to learn. To the classes, I left my home at 5 a.m. every Saturday and drove 3 hours to the cultural center. But this didn't stop me. After class, I practiced a lot. Through learning, I Chinese and joined in activities like Chinese . I visiting China.

I helped my classmates in many ways like and shopping in Chinese. This experience me to study further.

The British often say "Learning is a (追求)", which means "It is never too late to learn". I will keep on learning Chinese, hoping to be a lifelong learner. I believe that learning Chinese will bring even more to my life.

✅ 答案与逐段解析（点击可收起）
11. In March 2019. / He began to learn Chinese in March 2019.
12. His (Chinese) teacher Mr. Liu. / Mr. Liu (his Chinese teacher).
13. He is hardworking and never gives up. He has a lifelong love of learning.（言之有理即可）
14. （示例）I will try to learn a foreign language, because Jeff's story tells me it is never too late to learn.（开放题，合理即可）
15. 读万卷书不如行万里路。

逐段大意

第 1 段｜自我介绍：Jeff 2018 年退休时已会三门外语，最大的愿望是再学一门新语言。开篇点题。
第 2 段｜学中文的缘由与付出：被电视上的中国文化节目吸引，决定学中文；偶然在伦敦北部找到文化中心；每周六凌晨 5 点出发、开车 3 小时去上课；课后勤练，爱上中国诗歌，参加诗歌朗诵，越来越向往来中国。体现他勤奋、执着、热爱学习。
第 3 段｜中国之行：老师建议参加中国游学；2024 年 8 月与同学游西安，作为中文最好的同学担任口译员，帮大家用中文问路、购物；这段经历鼓励他继续深造。
第 4 段｜感悟与决心：引用英谚「Learning is a lifelong pursuit」，表示要继续学中文、做终身学习者，相信中文为人生带来更多可能。

重点句型与语法

I was so interested in it that I made the decision to learn Chinese.
be interested in；so + adj. + that；make a decision to do 决定做某事。
I found a cultural center in the north of London by chance.
by chance 偶然（= by accident）；in the north of … 在……北部。
It offered Chinese language courses fit for me.
offer sth. to sb. / offer sb. sth.；fit for sb. 适合某人（形容词短语作后置定语）。
To attend the classes, I left my home at 5 a.m. every Saturday and drove 3 hours to the cultural center.
To do 不定式作目的状语「为了上课」；every Saturday + 一般过去时表过去习惯动作。
Through learning, I developed a love for Chinese poems and joined in activities like Chinese poetry readings.
develop a love for 培养对……的热爱；join in 参加活动；like 作介词「例如」。
He suggested that I should take part in a study tour in China.
suggest that sb. (should) do 建议某人做某事（should 可省略）；take part in 参加活动。
During the trip, as the student best at Chinese among us, I tried to act as an interpreter.
as「作为」；best at 为 good at 的最高级；act as 充当、担任。
It's better to travel ten thousand miles than to read ten thousand books.（画线句 → 第 15 题）
It's better to do A than to do B. 做 A 比做 B 更好。译文：读万卷书不如行万里路。
Learning is a lifelong pursuit, which means "It is never too late to learn".
which 引导非限制性定语从句；It is never too late to learn. 活到老，学到老。
I will keep on learning Chinese, hoping to be a lifelong learner.
keep on doing 坚持做；hoping … 现在分词短语作伴随状语。
This experience encouraged me to study further.
encourage sb. to do 鼓励某人做某事；further「进一步地」。

Passage 3 · China — A Country of Etiquette

语法填空2025·吉林长春·中考真题 ／ 10 空 · 无提示词 3 个

China is a country of (礼仪). I'm working here as a foreign teacher and I'm leaving soon. I 　1　 (real) Chinese culture and society.

Everything was new to me when I first arrived 　2　 China. seats at the table, Chinese people have their own culture. Once, I 　3　 (invite) to a big dinner at a restaurant. Before the dinner, someone showed me to take the seat 　4　 was ready for the most important person. It faced the door.

I found another 　5　 (interest) thing. Chinese people like giving gifts . Last month, I 　6　 (have) a party with three friends in my home. They each brought two gifts. I open them, 　7　 one of my friends me not to do that . He thought it was 　8　 to open gifts in front of the givers.

These are amazing 　9　 (day) of cultural experiences. I believe my stay in China will not be 　10　 last time. I to come to China again.

✅ 答案与逐段解析（点击可收起）
1. really2. in3. was invited 4. that / which5. interesting6. had 7. but8. impolite9. days10. the

逐段大意与考点

第 1 段｜作者是在华外教，即将离开；他对中国文化和社会有深刻理解。
空 1 really：real (adj.) → really (adv.)，副词修饰动词 have。考点：词性转换。
第 2 段｜餐桌座位文化：初到中国一切全新；中国人讲究座次；作者曾被邀请赴宴，被领到面向大门、为最重要客人准备的座位。
空 2 in：arrive in + 国家/城市（大地点）。空 3 was invited：once 提示过去，且「被邀请」用一般过去时被动。空 4 that / which：定语从句修饰 the seat，先行词指物、在从句中作主语。
第 3 段｜送礼文化：中国人送礼成双；一次聚会三位朋友各带两件礼物；「我」迫不及待想拆，被朋友提醒别马上拆——当着送礼人面拆礼物不礼貌。
空 5 interesting：-ing 形容词修饰物。空 6 had：last month → 一般过去时。空 7 but：转折。空 8 impolite：由「提醒不要拆」推知不礼貌，im- 前缀表否定。
第 4 段｜总结：这段文化体验非常美妙，相信这不是最后一次来中国，期待再来。
空 9 days：these are → 名词复数。空 10 the：the last time「最后一次」，定冠词固定用法。

重点句型与语法

I really have a deep understanding of Chinese culture and society.
have a deep understanding of 对……有深刻理解。
As for seats at the table, Chinese people have their own culture.
as for 至于、关于；one's own + n. 某人自己的……。
Once, I was invited to a big dinner at a restaurant.
be invited to … 被邀请参加……；once「从前」常与一般过去时连用。
Someone showed me to take the seat that was ready for the most important person.
show sb. (to …) 领某人（到……）；be ready for 为……准备好；that 引导定语从句。
I couldn't wait to open them, but one of my friends reminded me not to do that right away.
can't wait to do 迫不及待做；remind sb. (not) to do 提醒某人（不要）做；right away 立刻；one of + 复数名词作主语，谓语用单数。
He thought it was impolite to open gifts in front of the givers.
it 作形式主语：It is + adj. + to do sth.；in front of 在……面前。
I believe my stay in China will not be the last time.
my stay in China（stay 作名词）；the last time 最后一次。
