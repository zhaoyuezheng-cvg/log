题型一 · 七选五

江西卷：减少塑料污染的五条建议

2025·江西·中考真题 ｜ 体裁：说明文 ｜ 主题：环境保护 ｜ 五个空格，从 A–G 七个选项中选出五个最佳答案

英文原文与选项Reading Text & Options

Plastic pollution is a serious problem, and it's getting worse. Plastic can stay for a long time. Scientists are trying to create plastic that breaks down quickly, but the best way for now is to use less plastic. 1

Stop using plastic bags. Instead, take your own reusable shopping bag to the store. Every year, people use tons of plastic bags worldwide. Some countries try to stop using plastic bags. 2

Say no to the straw (吸管). A study showed that around 8.3 billion plastic straws cover the world's beaches. So when you order a drink, say no to the straw, or bring your own reusable one.

3 Carry a reusable water bottle with you. Around the world, nearly a million plastic bottles are thrown away every minute. The problem is so bad that in some countries, stores aren't allowed to sell bottled water any more.

Avoid plastic packaging (包装). 4 It's almost everywhere. But there is something you can do, too: avoid fruit or vegetables packed in plastic.

Recycle what you can. You can't recycle all plastic things. 5 For example, you can recycle bottles and milk or juice bags. In some countries, machines take the bottles and give a little money back for each bottle.

  • A.They are fresh.
  • B.Don't buy bottled water.
  • C.Bottled water is expensive.
  • D.But it is possible to start small.
  • E.Here are five tips that can help you.
  • F.Other countries make customers pay for them.
  • G.Most things you buy come in plastic packaging.

答案速查Answer Key

七选五答案： 1E 2F 3B 4G 5D （多余干扰项：A、C）

逐题解析Detailed Analysis

1选 E — Here are five tips that can help you.（这里有五条可以帮到你的建议。）

推理空格位于首段段尾，前文提出问题"塑料污染严重、目前最好的办法是少用塑料"，紧接着后文用五个加粗小标题（Stop using…/Say no to…/Avoid…/Recycle…）分五条展开。E 项"以下是五条建议"正是承上启下的总起句，five tips 与五个段落一一对应。

知识点"总—分"结构说明文常考：首段末句多为引出下文的主题句，数字线索 five 是最强信号。

易错点不要看到前一句在讲科学家研制可降解塑料，就去选与"小处着手"有关的 D；D 是转折总结句，不适合统领五条建议。

2选 F — Other countries make customers pay for them.（另一些国家则让顾客为塑料袋付费。）

推理空格前一句是 Some countries try to stop using plastic bags.（一些国家设法停用塑料袋。）F 项以 Other countries…（另一些国家……） 与之呼应，构成 "Some countries… Other countries…" 的并列结构，介绍各国的不同做法。

知识点不定代词配对：some…, others/other…（一些……，另一些……）是阅读还原高频衔接信号；pay for sth. 为某物付钱。

易错点them 必须有复数名词可指代，此处指代 plastic bags，逻辑通顺；若选 A（They are fresh.），fresh"新鲜的"与塑料袋毫无关联。

3选 B — Don't buy bottled water.（不要买瓶装水。）

推理空格位于段首，需填主题句。本段后文都在讲"随身带可重复使用的水瓶、每分钟近百万个塑料瓶被丢弃、有些国家不再允许售卖瓶装水"。B 项"不要买瓶装水"精准概括全段。

知识点段首设空多考段落主旨题：由后文高频词 water bottle / plastic bottles / bottled water 归纳。

易混淆项 C（Bottled water is expensive. 瓶装水很贵）本段讲的是塑料瓶带来的环境问题与禁令，根本没有提到"价格贵"，故 C 为干扰项。

4选 G — Most things you buy come in plastic packaging.（你买的大多数东西都是塑料包装的。）

推理本段小标题为 Avoid plastic packaging（避免塑料包装），空格后一句是 It's almost everywhere.（它几乎无处不在。）G 项"你买的多数东西都用塑料包装"正是 It 指代的对象——plastic packaging，且解释了为什么说它"无处不在"。

知识点代词衔接：后文的 It 必须能在前句找到单数指代对象；词汇复现 packaging 也是重要线索。

易错点注意 packed（过去分词，"被包装的"）与 packaging（名词，"包装材料"）的区别，不要因为字形相近就误判句意。

5选 D — But it is possible to start small.（但是，从小事做起是可行的。）

推理空前句 You can't recycle all plastic things.（你不可能回收所有塑料制品。）语气令人无奈；D 项以 But 转折——"但从身边小事做起是可能的"，紧接着 For example 举出回收瓶子和牛奶/果汁袋等具体小事，逻辑严丝合缝。

知识点① 转折连词 But 标志语义由"不能做全部"转向"可以从点滴做起"；② 句型 It is possible to do sth.（做某事是可能的），it 为形式主语，真正主语是不定式。

易错点start small 是固定口语化表达，意为"从小处开始"，不能直译成"开始小"；若忽视 But 的转折作用，容易错选。

两个干扰项为何不选：A 项 They are fresh.（它们很新鲜）中的 fresh 在全文无对应语境，they 也无所指；C 项"瓶装水很贵"与第 3 段讨论的环保主题不符，属于利用 bottled water 一词设置的"词汇诱饵"。

题型二 · 阅读问答 + 句子翻译

湖南卷：退休老人 Jeff 的中文学习之路

2025·湖南·中考真题 ｜ 体裁：记叙文 ｜ 主题：终身学习 ｜ 11–14 题为简略回答问题，15 题将画线句译成中文

英文原文与题目Reading Text & Questions

My name is Jeff. I like learning foreign languages. When I retired (退休) in 2018, I could speak three foreign languages. At that time, my biggest wish was to learn a new one.

Later, I watched a program about Chinese culture on TV. I was so interested in it that I made the decision to learn Chinese. I found a cultural center in the north of London by chance. It offered Chinese language courses fit for me. In March 2019, I began to learn. To attend the classes, I left my home at 5 a.m. every Saturday and drove 3 hours to the cultural center. But this didn't stop me. After class, I practiced a lot. Through learning, I developed a love for Chinese poems and joined in activities like Chinese poetry readings. I increasingly dreamed of visiting China.

My Chinese teacher Mr. Liu always mentioned "It's better to travel ten thousand miles than to read ten thousand books". He suggested that I should take part in a study tour in China. So in August 2024, I visited Xi'an with my classmates of the cultural center. During the trip, as the student best at Chinese among us, I tried to act as an interpreter (口译员). I helped my classmates in many ways like asking the way and shopping in Chinese. This experience encouraged me to study further.

The British often say "Learning is a lifelong pursuit (追求)", which means "It is never too late to learn". I will keep on learning Chinese, hoping to be a lifelong learner. I believe that learning Chinese will bring even more possibilities to my life.

  1. When did Jeff begin to learn Chinese?
  2. Who suggested a study tour in China?
  3. What do you think of Jeff?
  4. What new things will you try after reading Jeff's story? Why?
  5. 将短文中画线部分翻译成中文。

逐题解析Detailed Analysis

11When did Jeff begin to learn Chinese?（杰夫什么时候开始学中文的？）

参考答案In March 2019.（2019 年 3 月。）完整答法：He began to learn Chinese in March 2019.

定位第二段原句：In March 2019, I began to learn. 此处的 learn 承接上文，即 learn Chinese。

知识点When 提问用时间介词短语回答：月份/年份前用 in（in March 2019）；具体某一天才用 on。

易错点不要答成 2018 年——2018 是他"退休"的时间；2019 年 3 月才是开始学中文的时间。

12Who suggested a study tour in China?（谁建议去中国研学旅行？）

参考答案(Jeff's Chinese teacher) Mr. Liu. 完整答法：Jeff's Chinese teacher Mr. Liu suggested a study tour in China.

定位第三段：My Chinese teacher Mr. Liu always mentioned… He suggested that I should take part in a study tour in China. He 即指 Mr. Liu。

知识点suggest (that) sb. (should) do sth. 建议某人做某事，宾语从句用虚拟语气（should + 动词原形，should 可省略）。

易错点不能答 Jeff 自己——文中是他的中文老师刘老师建议的；注意人名书写 Mr. Liu 首字母大写。

13What do you think of Jeff?（你觉得杰夫是个怎样的人？）— 开放性评价题

参考答案He is hard-working / determined / brave / never too old to learn.（他很勤奋／有毅力／勇敢／活到老学到老。言之有理即可）

依据退休后仍坚持学新语言（lifelong learner）；每周六凌晨 5 点出发、驱车 3 小时上课（determined 坚定的）；课后大量练习、主动当口译员（hard-working / helpful / confident）。

知识点评价人物题用形容词作答：great, amazing, hard-working, strong-willed, warm-hearted, full of love for learning 等均可。

易错点必须使用描述人物的形容词并能在文中找到依据，不要只复述事件；语法上以 He is… 开头，避免只写一个动词词组。

14What new things will you try after reading Jeff's story? Why?（读了杰夫的故事后，你会尝试什么新事物？为什么？）— 开放性表达题

示范答案I will try to learn a new foreign language / take up a new hobby. Because Jeff's story tells me it is never too late to learn, and learning can bring more possibilities to my life.

得分要点本题须两问两答：① 用一般将来时（will + 动词原形）说出一件新事物；② 用 Because… 说明理由，理由尽量回扣文章主题（终身学习、坚持、带来可能性）。

易错点时态用一般现在时、或只答 what 不答 why 都会失分；新事物要具体合理，不可照抄与题目无关的句子。

15将画线部分翻译成中文：It's better to travel ten thousand miles than to read ten thousand books.

参考译文读万卷书，不如行万里路。（行万里路胜过读万卷书。）

结构本句是 It's better to do A than to do B 句型："做 A 比做 B 更好"，it 是形式主语，真正主语是两个不定式短语 to travel… 与 to read…。

知识点① better 是 well 的比较级，than 引出比较对象；② travel ten thousand miles 行一万里路；③ ten thousand = 一万（英语中没有"万"这个单位）。

易错点than 后连接的动词要与前面保持一致，用不定式 to read，不能写成 read；better 的"更"义不能漏译，也不要译成"旅行比读书好"而丢掉数量和文采。

题型三 · 语法填空

吉林长春卷：一位外教眼中的中国礼仪

2025·吉林长春·中考真题 ｜ 体裁：记叙文 ｜ 主题：文化礼仪 ｜ 在空白处填入 1 个适当的单词或括号内单词的正确形式

英文原文（含空）Cloze Text

China is a country of etiquette (礼仪). I'm working here as a foreign teacher and I'm leaving soon. I 1 (real) have a deep understanding of Chinese culture and society.

Everything was new to me when I first arrived 2 China. As for seats at the table, Chinese people have their own culture. Once, I 3 (invite) to a big dinner at a restaurant. Before the dinner, someone showed me to take the seat 4 was ready for the most important person. It faced the door.

I found another 5 (interest) thing. Chinese people like giving gifts in pairs. Last month, I 6 (have) a party with three friends in my home. They each brought two gifts. I couldn't wait to open them, 7 one of my friends reminded me not to do that right away. He thought it was 8 (polite) to open gifts in front of the givers.

These are amazing 9 (day) of cultural experiences. I believe my stay in China will not be 10 last time. I expect to come to China again.

答案速查Answer Key

语法填空： 1really 2in 3was invited 4that / which 5interesting 6had 7but 8impolite 9days 10the

逐题解析Detailed Analysis

1really（real → really，副词）

推理空格修饰动词 have，须用副词：I really have a deep understanding…（我对中国文化与社会有着很深的理解）。

知识点形容词 + ly → 副词，副词修饰动词、形容词或全句。

易错点不能填形容词 real；注意 real → really 直接加 ly，无需去 e。

2in（介词填空）

推理arrive 后接地名大地点 China，固定搭配 arrive in + 大地点。

知识点arrive in + 城市/国家等大地点；arrive at + 车站、机场、小村镇等小地点；get to + 地点。

易错点arrive 是不及物动词，不能直接加宾语；误用 at 搭配国家名是常见错误。

3was invited（一般过去时的被动语态）

推理主语 I 是"邀请"动作的承受者（我被邀请参加晚宴），故用被动语态；由 Once（有一次）及上下文可知是过去时，I 对应单数 be 动词，故为 was invited。

知识点一般过去时被动语态：was/were + 过去分词；invite → invited。

易错点不要看到主语是 I 就习惯性填 invited（主动语态）；被动语态的 be 动词不能漏。

4that / which（定语从句关系代词）

推理先行词是 the seat（座位，指物），关系词在从句 ____ was ready for the most important person 中作主语，故用关系代词 that 或 which。句意：有人领我坐在为最重要的人准备的座位上。

知识点先行词为物、关系代词在从句中作主语时用 that/which，且不可省略。

易错点作主语不能用关系副词 where（where 在从句中作地点状语）；也不能漏填关系词。

5interesting（interest → interesting，形容词）

推理空格修饰名词 thing，需形容词：another interesting thing（另一件有趣的事）。

知识点-ing 形容词表示"令人……的"，多修饰事物（interesting, surprising, exciting）；-ed 形容词表示"感到……的"，多描述人（interested, surprised）。

易错点thing 是物，应用 interesting；写 interested 修饰事物是高频错点。

6had（have → had，一般过去时）

推理时间状语 Last month（上个月）明确指向一般过去时，have 的过去式为不规则变化 had。

知识点have a party 举办聚会；have → had → had 不规则变化需牢记。

易错点不能写 have / has；有明确过去时间状语时优先锁定过去时。

7but（并列连词）

推理前半句"我迫不及待想打开礼物"，后半句"一个朋友提醒我不要立刻打开"——前后语义转折，故填 but。

知识点can't wait to do sth. 迫不及待做某事；remind sb. not to do sth. 提醒某人不要做某事；but 表转折，and 表顺承，so 表结果。

易错点若填 and 则无法体现"想打开却被劝阻"的转折逻辑。

8impolite（polite → impolite，加否定前缀）

推理结合上文朋友提醒"不要当着送礼人的面立刻打开"，说明在中国人看来这种做法是"不礼貌的"，故用 polite 的反义词 impolite。句型 it is/was + 形容词 + to do sth. 中 it 为形式主语。

知识点常见否定前缀：im-（用在 p/b/m 前，如 impolite, impossible）、un-（unhappy）、in-（incorrect）。

易错点若不看语境只按提示词写 polite，语义正好相反；前缀要双写 m 吗？不双写——im + polite = impolite。

9days（day → days，名词复数）

推理主语 These（这些）与 be 动词 are 均提示复数，day 的复数直接加 s：These are amazing days…

知识点可数名词单复数与主语、be 动词保持一致；元音字母 + y 结尾直接加 s（day → days）。

易错点day 以元音 a + y 结尾，不能变 y 为 i 加 es（那是辅音字母 + y 的规则，如 story → stories）。

10the（定冠词）

推理the last time 是固定表达，意为"最后一次"，序数意味的 last 前通常加定冠词；句意：我相信我的中国之行不会是最后一次。

知识点the last/next + 名词 表特指顺序；固定搭配 for the last time（最后一次）。

易错点不能填 a 或不填；注意区分 the last time（最后一次）与 a long time（很长时间）。

📐 本篇语法点总复盘

  • 词形变化：形容词↔副词（real→really）、-ing/-ed 形容词（interesting/interested）、否定前缀（polite→impolite）、名词复数（day→days）。
  • 动词语态时态：一般过去时被动语态 was/were done；一般过去时 had。
  • 从句与连词：that/which 引导的定语从句（先行词为物、作主语）；but 表转折。
  • 虚词：介词 arrive in；定冠词 the last time。

一、江西卷 · 减少塑料污染（23 个）

plastic/ˈplæstɪk/n. / adj.塑料；塑料的形容词、名词同形；plastic bag 塑料袋，plastic bottle 塑料瓶。We should use fewer plastic bags.我们应该少用塑料袋。
pollution/pəˈluːʃn/n.污染（不可数）动词 pollute（污染）→ 名词 pollution；air/water pollution 空气/水污染。Air pollution is a big problem in big cities.空气污染在大城市是个大问题。
serious/ˈsɪəriəs/adj.严重的；严肃的副词 seriously；a serious problem 一个严重的问题。This is a serious mistake.这是一个严重的错误。
create/kriˈeɪt/v.创造；创建；研制名词 creation / creator；create jobs 创造就业机会。Writers create many interesting characters.作家创造出许多有趣的人物。
reusable/ˌriːˈjuːzəbl/adj.可重复使用的re-（再、重新）+ use（使用）+ -able（能……的）；反义词 disposable 一次性的。Take a reusable water bottle with you.随身携带一个可重复使用的水瓶。
worldwide/ˌwɜːldˈwaɪd/adv. / adj.全世界；遍及全球的world（世界）+ wide（广阔的），可作副词也可作形容词。The song became famous worldwide.这首歌在全世界出了名。
straw/strɔː/n.吸管；稻草本文指"吸管"；a plastic straw 塑料吸管。She drank juice through a straw.她用吸管喝果汁。
billion/ˈbɪljən/num.十亿8.3 billion 83 亿；前面有具体数字时 billion 不加 s。There are over eight billion people in the world.世界上有八十多亿人口。
nearly/ˈnɪəli/adv.几乎；差不多相当于 almost，常与数字连用。It took nearly two hours to get there.到那儿花了将近两个小时。
allow/əˈlaʊ/v.允许；准许allow sb. to do sth. 允许某人做某事；be allowed to do 被允许做。Students are not allowed to use phones in class.学生上课时不允许使用手机。
bottled water/ˈbɒtld ˈwɔːtə/n.瓶装水bottled 是 bottle 的过去分词作形容词，"装瓶的"。Bottled water creates a lot of plastic waste.瓶装水产生大量塑料垃圾。
packaging/ˈpækɪdʒɪŋ/n.包装材料；外包装动词 pack（包装）→ packaging；plastic packaging 塑料包装。Too much packaging is bad for the environment.过度包装对环境有害。
tip/tɪp/n.建议；提示；小费本文义为"实用建议"，常用复数 tips；a useful tip 一条有用的建议。Here are some tips for learning English.这里有一些学英语的小建议。
customer/ˈkʌstəmə/n.顾客；客户custom（惠顾）+ -er 表示人；商店、餐厅的顾客。The shop always treats its customers well.这家店总是对顾客很好。
fresh/freʃ/adj.新鲜的；清新的fresh fruit/vegetables 新鲜水果/蔬菜；fresh air 新鲜空气。The vegetables in this market are very fresh.这个市场的蔬菜很新鲜。
possibleadj./ˈpɒsəbl/可能的句型 It is possible to do sth. 做某事是可能的；反义词 impossible。It is possible to finish the work today.今天完成这项工作是可能的。
break downphr.分解；（机器）出故障本文指塑料"分解、降解"；还可指汽车等抛锚。Some plastics take hundreds of years to break down.有些塑料需要数百年才能降解。
for nowphr.目前；暂时相当于 at the moment / for the time being。That's enough for now.目前这就够了。
tons ofphr.大量的；许多（吨）ton 意为"吨"，tons of 口语中表示"大量"，后接可数或不可数名词。I have tons of homework to do tonight.我今晚有一大堆作业要做。
say no to sth.phr.拒绝某物；对……说不反义表达 say yes to；常用于倡议类文章。Say no to junk food.向垃圾食品说不。
throw awayphr.扔掉；丢弃动副短语，代词宾语放中间：throw it away。Don't throw away the old books.别把旧书扔掉。
not… any morephr.不再……多用于美式英语；英式常写 not…anymore；表示次数不再增加。He doesn't live here any more.他不再住在这里了。
start smallphr.从小处做起；从小事开始small 在此作副词/主语补足语，整体为固定表达。You don't need big plans — just start small.你不需要宏大的计划——就从小事做起。

二、湖南卷 · Jeff 学中文（23 个）

foreign/ˈfɒrən/adj.外国的；对外的foreign language 外语；foreigner n. 外国人（注意 g 不发音）。Can you speak any foreign languages?你会说外语吗？
retire/rɪˈtaɪə/v.退休；退役retire from… 从……退休；retired adj. 退休了的。My grandfather retired at the age of 60.我祖父 60 岁时退休了。
program/ˈprəʊɡræm/n.节目；程序；计划英式也写作 programme；a TV program 电视节目。I watched a program about animals last night.我昨晚看了一个关于动物的节目。
culture/ˈkʌltʃə/n.文化cultural adj. 文化的；cultural center 文化中心。I'm interested in Chinese culture.我对中国文化感兴趣。
make a decision/dɪˈsɪʒn/phr.做决定；下定决心decide v. → decision n.；make a decision to do sth. 决定做某事。I made a decision to get up early.我下定决心早起。
cultural centern.文化中心culture 的形容词 cultural + center；类似 sports center 体育中心。She takes dance classes at the cultural center.她在文化中心上舞蹈课。
by chancephr.偶然；碰巧同义：by accident / accidentally；反义 on purpose 故意地。I met my old friend by chance in the street.我在街上偶然遇见了老朋友。
offer/ˈɒfə/v. / n.提供；主动给予offer sth. (to sb.) 提供某物；offer to do sth. 主动提出做某事。The school offers free lunch to students.学校向学生提供免费午餐。
course/kɔːs/n.课程；过程take a course 修一门课；of course 当然；language courses 语言课程。I want to take a Chinese course this summer.今年夏天我想修一门中文课。
attend/əˈtend/v.出席；参加（课程、会议）attend a class/meeting 上课/参加会议，强调到场；attend school 上学。All students must attend the meeting.所有学生都必须参加这次会议。
develop/dɪˈveləp/v.培养；发展；开发develop a love/habit of… 培养……的热爱/习惯；development n.He developed a love for music at an early age.他很小的时候就培养起了对音乐的热爱。
poem/ˈpəʊɪm/n.诗；诗歌（具体作品）poet n. 诗人；poetry n. 诗歌（总称，不可数）。Li Bai wrote many famous poems.李白写了许多著名的诗。
join inphr.参加（活动）join in + 活动，相当于 take part in；join + 组织/人。Can I join in the game?我能参加这个游戏吗？
increasingly/ɪnˈkriːsɪŋli/adv.越来越……；日益相当于 more and more，修饰形容词或动词。It is becoming increasingly difficult to find a job.找工作变得越来越难了。
dream of doing sth.phr.梦想做某事of 是介词，后接名词或动词-ing；也可写 dream about。She dreams of becoming a doctor.她梦想成为一名医生。
mention/ˈmenʃn/v.提到；说起mention sth. to sb. 向某人提起某事；Don't mention it. 不客气。He mentioned the trip to me yesterday.他昨天向我提起了那次旅行。
take part inphr.参加（活动并发挥作用）后接活动、比赛、运动等；与 join in 近义。Will you take part in the singing competition?你会参加歌唱比赛吗？
act asphr.担任；充当as 为介词，后接身份类名词，名词前常不用冠词。A friend acted as our guide.一位朋友充当了我们的向导。
interpreter/ɪnˈtɜːprɪtə/n.口译员；口译者interpret v. 口译；translator 多指笔译者。She works as an interpreter in a big company.她在一家大公司做口译员。
experience/ɪkˈspɪəriəns/n. / v.经历（可数）；经验（不可数）；体验指具体"经历"时可数，指"经验"时不可数；experienced adj. 有经验的。The trip to Xi'an was an unforgettable experience.西安之行是一次难忘的经历。
encourage/ɪnˈkʌrɪdʒ/v.鼓励；激励encourage sb. to do sth. 鼓励某人做某事；名词 encouragement。My teacher encourages me to speak more English.老师鼓励我多说英语。
lifelong/ˈlaɪflɒŋ/adj.终身的；毕生的life（生命）+ long（长的）；lifelong learning 终身学习。Reading is my lifelong hobby.阅读是我终身的爱好。
keep on doing sth.phr.继续/坚持做某事on 是介词，后接动词-ing；同义 go on doing。Keep on trying and you will succeed.继续努力，你就会成功。

三、长春卷 · 外教眼中的中国礼仪（16 个）

etiquette/ˈetɪket/n.礼仪；礼节（不可数）a country of etiquette 礼仪之邦；table etiquette 餐桌礼仪。China is a country of etiquette.中国是一个礼仪之邦。
deep/diːp/adj.深的；深厚的a deep understanding 深刻的理解；deeply adv. 深深地。He has a deep love for his family.他深爱着他的家人。
society/səˈsaɪəti/n.社会social adj. 社会的；in modern society 在现代社会中。Rules help our society work well.规则帮助我们的社会良好运转。
arrive in / at/əˈraɪv/phr.到达……（in + 大地点 / at + 小地点）arrive 为不及物动词，不能直接加宾语；同义 get to、reach（及物）。We arrived in Beijing on Monday morning.我们星期一早上到达北京。
face/feɪs/v. / n.面对；面朝 / 脸文中作动词"面朝"：The seat faces the door. 座位对着门。My bedroom faces the park.我的卧室面朝公园。
in pairsphr.成对地；成双地pair n. 一双、一对；work in pairs 两人一组练习。Please practice the dialogue in pairs.请两人一组练习这个对话。
remind/rɪˈmaɪnd/v.提醒；使想起remind sb. (not) to do sth. 提醒某人（不要）做某事；remind sb. of sth. 使某人想起。Please remind me to call Mum.请提醒我给妈妈打电话。
amazing/əˈmeɪzɪŋ/adj.令人惊叹的；了不起的amaze v. 使惊奇；-ing 形容词修饰事物，amazed 修饰人。What an amazing show it is!多么精彩的演出啊！
expect/ɪkˈspekt/v.期待；预料expect to do sth. 期待做某事；expect sb. to do sth. 期待某人做某事。I expect to see you soon.我期待很快见到你。
right awayphr.立刻；马上同义 at once / immediately / right now。Please come here right away.请马上到这里来。
be ready forphr.为……做好准备后接名词/代词；be ready to do sth. 准备好做某事。The room is ready for the guests.房间已经为客人准备好了。
show sb. to…phr.领某人到……to 为介词表方向；show sb. around 带某人参观。The waiter showed us to our table.服务员把我们领到了餐桌旁。
in front ofphr.在……（外部的）前面区别 in the front of 在……（内部的）前部。There is a tree in front of the classroom.教室前面有一棵树。
can't wait to dophr.迫不及待做某事wait for sb./sth. 等待；can't help doing 忍不住做（注意区分）。I can't wait to open the presents.我迫不及待地想打开礼物。
as forphr.至于；关于用于转换话题，放句首，后接名词/代词/动名词。As for me, I like tea better.至于我，我更喜欢茶。
impolite/ˌɪmpəˈlaɪt/adj.不礼貌的（polite + 否定前缀 im-）同类：impossible 不可能的；im- 多用在 p/b/m 开头的词前。It is impolite to talk with your mouth full.嘴里塞满东西说话是不礼貌的。
