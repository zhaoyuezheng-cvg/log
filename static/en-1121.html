Michael Platte, 1 12-year-old boy, is from the USA. He likes making cupcakes. His grandma is his teacher. Now he's good at making all kinds of cupcakes. He always makes 2 (health) cupcakes on Sunday afternoon and takes some to his classmates the next morning. They say the cupcakes 3 (be) good.

答案：1. a   2. healthy   3. are   4. standing   5. but   6. also   7. sells   8. his   9. acts   10. children
解析：
1. a. 泛指一个12岁的男孩，且 12 (twelve) 以辅音音素开头，用不定冠词 a。
2. healthy. 修饰名词 cupcakes 需用形容词，health 的形容词是 healthy。
3. are. 主语 the cupcakes 是复数，be 动词用 are。
4. standing. see sb. doing sth. 看见某人正在做某事，用现在分词。
5. but. 前后句意转折（喜欢蛋糕但没钱），用 but。
6. also. 表示“也”，用于肯定句中，放在实义动词之前。
7. sells. 主语 Michael 是第三人称单数，一般现在时用 sells。
8. his. 修饰名词 act，用形容词性物主代词 his。
9. acts. 结合语境，此处应填名词复数 acts（善举）。
10. children. those 后接可数名词复数，child 的复数是 children。
