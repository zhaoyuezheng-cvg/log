阅读短文 · 宁波博物馆 阅读短文，根据要求回答下面各小题。

主题：人与社会——文化场馆 体裁：说明文 词数：约 130 难度 ★★☆☆☆ 答案见下

Ningbo Museum is in the city of Ningbo, Zhejiang. More people know the museum after it appeared in the TV series Three-Body (《三体》).

Ningbo Museum looks like mountains. But if you see it in the sky, it looks like a large boat. It is one of the symbols of the city.

The museum isn’t very old. It opened to visitors on December 5th, 2008. In the museum, you can learn about the culture, history and art of Ningbo. The museum has a collection of more than 60,000 exhibits（展品）. If you want to learn about Ningbo, just start from Ningbo Museum.

Ningbo Museum is open from Tuesday to Sunday. Visitors can stay in it between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm. But after 4:00 pm, they can’t go into the museum.

Across from the museum is the Yinzhou Park. It is a popular place in Ningbo, too. With its trees, flowers and water, the park is a place for people to relax. It’s a good idea to visit the park after leaving Ningbo Museum.

宁波博物馆位于浙江省宁波市。自从它出现在电视剧《三体》中后，更多人知道了这座博物馆。

宁波博物馆看起来像群山。但从空中看，它像一艘大船。它是这座城市的象征之一。

这座博物馆不算很古老。它于 2008 年 12 月 5 日对游客开放。在博物馆里，你可以了解宁波的文化、历史和艺术。馆藏展品超过 6 万件。想了解宁波，就从宁波博物馆开始吧。

宁波博物馆从周二到周日开放。游客可以在上午 9 点到下午 5 点之间停留。但下午 4 点以后就不能进入了。

博物馆对面是鄞州公园。它在宁波也很受欢迎。这里有树、有花、有水，是人们放松的好去处。离开宁波博物馆后去公园走走是个好主意。

Zootopia 2 came out on November 26th, 2025. Many kids are very excited about it. Judy (a clever rabbit) and Nick (a smart fox) are back. They are good partners and work as police officers. The slow sloth（树懒） named Flash also comes back. It still amuses with slow words and fast driving.

In this new story, Judy and Nick meet a new strange reptile（爬行动物） — Gary, a snake. Gary’s coming brings a big problem to Zootopia. They find a long-time secret and have to work quietly to solve it. The film’s pictures are much better than the first film’s. The animals’ hair and scales（鳞片） look very real. You can watch it in many ways, like 3D.

For Chinese fans, there are surprises. Some famous Chinese voice actors（配音演员） join in. There is a 3-minute special part about “Panda Lane (熊猫巷)” with Chinese style. Zhou Shen sings the Chinese version of the theme song, which sounds great.

Zootopia 2 is fit for all ages. It tells a story about friendship and courage（勇气）. After watching it, you’ll feel warm and full of positive energy（正能量）. It will be a nice experience and you can go to the cinema with your family to enjoy this wonderful adventure and share happy moments with your family members.

阅读理解 C · Maya 的研学之旅 从每小题所给的 A、B、C、D 四个选项中选出一个最佳选项。

主题：人与自然——环保研学 体裁：记叙文 词数：约 150 难度 ★★★☆☆ 答案见下

It was a cold Monday morning, but for Maya and the other seventy-four students, it was a good day to go on a trip to the Lower Wapato Creek（小溪）. It is home to many kinds of trees and salmon（鲑鱼）.

Why did those students take the trip to the creek? It was because of a letter from Maya. In her letter, Maya said, “I like salmon. To help them, I want to plant trees at the Lower Wapato Creek Habitat（栖息地）.”

After reading Maya’s letter, her teacher Lauren helped make her wish come true. At first, the students planted trees along the creek. It was difficult but interesting for them. Then a biologist（生物学家） took them to visit salmon. She gave them a lesson about salmon.

Maya felt very happy to take the great trip. She learned a lot about salmon and planted trees to help them. “I want to show those trees we planted to my friends and parents,” she said happily.

书面表达 · My School Trip to Qufu 根据提示写一篇英语短文，介绍一次曲阜研学之旅。

主题：人与社会——研学旅行 体裁：记叙文 建议词数：80—100 难度 ★★★☆☆ 范文见下
✍️ 写作要点：①去哪儿 ②什么时候去 ③和谁去 ④做了/看到/吃了什么 ⑤感受如何 ⑥学到了什么。
📌 参考词汇：the Three Kong Sites 三孔｜the statue of Confucius 孔子像｜Confucius Museum 孔子博物馆｜traditional culture 传统文化｜Qufu Normal University 曲阜师范大学｜campfire party 篝火晚会｜unforgettable 难忘的。

My School Trip to Qufu

Last term, I went on a school trip to Qufu with my teachers and classmates. It was a wonderful three-day trip and really unforgettable.

On the first day, we visited the statue of Confucius. We listened to his stories and learned about his great thoughts.

On the second day, we visited the Three Kong Sites. We saw many ancient buildings and experienced traditional culture there. In the evening, we had a campfire party. We sang and danced happily. We were so excited.

On the third day, we visited Confucius Museum. A guide showed us around and we saw many old works. Also, we visited Qufu Normal University. We walked around the campus and felt its wonderful school life. It encouraged me to study hard to get into a good university in the future.

All in all, I learned much about Chinese traditional culture from this trip. It was truly a meaningful trip. It was a day to remember.

我的曲阜研学之旅

上学期，我和老师、同学们一起去曲阜研学。那是一次精彩的三天之旅，真的很难忘。

第一天，我们参观了孔子像。我们听了他的故事，了解了他伟大的思想。

第二天，我们参观了三孔。我们在那里看到了许多古建筑，体验了传统文化。晚上，我们开了篝火晚会，开心地唱歌跳舞，特别兴奋。

第三天，我们参观了孔子博物馆。一位导游带我们参观，我们看到了许多古老的作品。我们还参观了曲阜师范大学，在校园里散步，感受美好的校园生活。这激励我努力学习，将来考上一所好大学。

总之，我从这次旅行中学到了很多中国传统文化。这真是一次有意义的旅行。这是一个值得铭记的日子。
