Movie name: My Neighbour Totoro

B

三国少年谈节日与时差 阅读下面短文，从每题 A、B、C 三个选项中选出最佳答案。

主题：人与社会——文化习俗 语篇类型：记叙文 词数：176 难度 ★★★★☆ 建议用时 6′ 正确率 △
Calvin（新西兰）

I'm Calvin and I come from New Zealand. Our Christmas is in summer, so we have beach parties under the stars. Christmas here is really different from Christmas in places where it snows.

Maria（澳大利亚）

I, Maria, come from Australia. People in China are celebrating the Spring Festival and family members are reuniting for a New Year's Eve dinner, while people in Australia may be taking a walk to enjoy the cool summer night or preparing for their New Year celebrations. This gives me a deep understanding of how time zones affect people's daily life.

Huck（西班牙）

I, Huck, am from Spain. Now I'll tell you about siesta（午休） time—our special break in the middle of the day. We sit down with family for a relaxing meal, unlike the quick lunches in other places. We usually have lunch and have a nap（小睡） from 2:00 to 5:00 in the afternoon. And at night, Barcelona really comes to life! The sun sets late, so we hold parties and enjoy ourselves at midnight.

卡尔文（新西兰）：我是卡尔文，来自新西兰。我们的圣诞节在夏天，所以我们在星空下举办海滩派对。这里的圣诞节和下雪地方的圣诞节很不一样。

玛丽亚（澳大利亚）：我是玛丽亚，来自澳大利亚。中国人正在庆祝春节、家人团聚吃年夜饭，而澳大利亚人可能正在散步享受凉爽的夏夜，或者在为他们的新年庆祝活动做准备。这让我对时区如何影响人们的日常生活有了深刻的理解。

赫克（西班牙）：我是赫克，来自西班牙。现在我来给你讲讲午休时间——我们一天中间特殊的休息。我们和家人坐下来享用一顿轻松的饭菜，不像其他地方那样匆匆吃个快餐。我们通常吃午饭，下午 2 点到 5 点小睡一会儿。到了晚上，巴塞罗那真正活跃起来！太阳落山很晚，所以我们举办聚会，在午夜玩得开心。

C

为 Nick 义演筹款 阅读下面短文，从每题 A、B、C 三个选项中选出最佳答案。

主题：人与社会——公益事业 语篇类型：记叙文 词数：162 难度 ★★★☆☆ 建议用时 7′ 正确率 △

It is Saturday today. Steven doesn't need to go to school. But he and his classmates still meet at the school gate at 8:00 in the morning. Why? Because they have to go to the City Park.

They take a bus and get to the park at 8:30 a.m. At 9 o'clock, the students are giving a concert. Steven is playing the piano. He does it well. Diana, Steven's deskmate, is singing an English song. Jenny is good at playing the violin, and she is playing it now. David is playing the drums. Many people in the park are listening to their music.

Why do the children give this concert? They do it for Nick, a boy in their class. Nick is ill in hospital. The students want to help him. They give this concert to raise money for him. They hope Nick can get well and come back to school early.
