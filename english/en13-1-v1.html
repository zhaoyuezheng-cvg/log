Min Sun and the Reed Clothes

Min Sun was a man in the State of Lu. He was a student of Confucius. Among all students of Confucius, he was famous for moral conduct（德行）.

Min Sun's mother passed away early. Later his father took another wife. She gave birth to two sons. The stepmother wasn't good to Min Sun. In winter, two younger brothers wore winter clothes made of cotton, but Min Sun only wore clothes with reed cattins（芦苇）.

One day, Min Sun followed his father to go out. He accidentally（偶然地） dropped something onto the ground. Then his father hit him. The reed cattins flew out from the broken seam（接缝） of the thin clothes, and then his father knew that his wife wasn't good to Min Sun.

After the father came back home, he wanted his wife to leave Min Sun's home. Min Sun fell to his knees and asked his father to forgive（原谅） the stepmother. He said, "If Mother stays at home, only I myself have to feel cold. But if you drive Mother away, all three children will have to feel cold."

His father was deeply moved and took Min Sun's advice. The stepmother heard of this, felt sorry and began to be good to Min Sun. And she treated Min Sun as her own son.

参考译文 · 闵子骞与芦花衣

闵子骞是鲁国人，是孔子的学生。在孔子的所有学生中，他以德行著称。

闵子骞的母亲很早就去世了。后来父亲又娶了一位妻子，她生了两个儿子。继母对闵子骞不好。冬天，两个弟弟穿着棉做的冬衣，闵子骞却只穿着芦苇絮的衣服。

一天，闵子骞随父亲出门，不小心把东西掉在地上。父亲打了他，芦苇絮从薄衣破裂的接缝处飞了出来，父亲这才知道妻子对闵子骞不好。

父亲回家后想让妻子离开闵家。闵子骞跪下来请求父亲原谅继母，说："如果母亲留在家里，只有我一个人受冷；但若把母亲赶走，三个孩子都要挨冻。"

父亲深受感动，听从了闵子骞的劝告。继母听说后十分懊悔，从此对闵子骞很好，把他当作亲儿子对待。

The Magic of World Book Day

23 April is World Book and Copyright Day. It was set up by UNESCO in 1995 to encourage people around the world to enjoy reading and respect the creators of books.

This special day was chosen to honour some of the world's most famous writers, like William Shakespeare and Miguel de Cervantes, who both passed away on this day. It also connects to a beautiful tradition from Catalonia, Spain, where a brave knight（骑士） named George received a book as a gift, symbolizing courage and wisdom. Today, this tradition has become a custom where people exchange books and roses. The book stands for wisdom, and the rose stands for love.

On World Book Day, many schools and libraries hold fun activities to promote（促进） reading. These can include reading marathons, book fairs, and storytelling events. It's a day to remind us that books are like keys that open doors to new worlds. Through books, we can learn about animals, science, history, and different cultures.

参考译文 · 世界读书日的魔力

4 月 23 日是世界图书与版权日。它由联合国教科文组织于 1995 年设立，旨在鼓励全世界的人享受阅读、尊重书籍创作者。

选定这一天是为了纪念一些世界最著名的作家，如威廉·莎士比亚和米格尔·德·塞万提斯——他们都是在这一天去世的。它还与西班牙加泰罗尼亚的一个美丽传统有关：一位名叫乔治的勇敢骑士得到一本书作为礼物，象征着勇气与智慧。如今这一传统已成习俗——人们互赠图书与玫瑰。书代表智慧，玫瑰代表爱。

在世界读书日，许多学校和图书馆举办有趣的活动来促进阅读，包括阅读马拉松、书展和讲故事活动。这一天提醒我们：书就像打开新世界大门的钥匙。通过书，我们可以了解动物、科学、历史和不同的文化。

Yang Chen-Ning—A Great Scientist

Yang Chen-Ning was a world-famous physicist and a Nobel Prize winner. He passed away in Beijing on 18 October, 2025, when he was 103 years old. As a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and a teacher at Tsinghua University, 1F because of his great contributions.

Yang was born in Hefei, Anhui in 1922. He had a wonderful study life. First, he studied at famous universities in China. Later, 2B, and got his PhD from the University of Chicago. In 1957, he worked with his partner Tung-Dao Lee and put forward the theory of parity non-conservation in weak interaction. 3A, they won the Nobel Prize in Physics. They became the first Chinese to get this prize. Yang's "Yang-Mills Theory" is also seen as an important part of modern physics.

Yang loved his motherland deeply. In 1971, he returned to China for the first time since 1945. After that, he helped build links between Chinese and American scientists. He gave advice on China's scientific development, raised money for Chinese scholars to study abroad, and in 1999, 4D. He worked hard to train young talent and help develop basic scientific research in China.

Yang Chen-Ning lived a long life of 103 years. He was a legend who explored the unknown and served his country. 5E will always encourage young people to work hard for science and national development.

参考译文 · 杨振宁——伟大的科学家

杨振宁是世界著名的物理学家、诺贝尔物理学奖得主。2025 年 10 月 18 日，他在北京逝世，享年 103 岁。作为中国科学院院士和清华大学教师，他因巨大贡献在大学的讣告中被誉为"不朽"。

杨振宁 1922 年生于安徽合肥，求学之路精彩纷呈。他先在中国著名大学学习，后来赴美深造，获芝加哥大学博士学位。1957 年，他与合作伙伴李政道提出弱相互作用中宇称不守恒理论，并因此获得诺贝尔物理学奖，成为最早获此奖的中国人。他的"杨-米尔斯理论"也被视为现代物理的重要组成部分。

杨振宁深深热爱祖国。1971 年，他自 1945 年后首次回国。此后，他助力搭建中美科学家之间的桥梁，为中国科学发展建言献策，为中国学者出国深造筹措经费，并于 1999 年回到清华大学。他努力培养青年人才，助力中国基础科学研究的发展。

杨振宁享年 103 岁。他是探索未知、报效祖国的传奇人物。他追求真理、热爱祖国的精神将永远激励年轻人为科学与国家发展而奋斗。
