阅读理解 A · 时区（Time Zones） 阅读下面短文，从每题 A、B、C 三个选项中选出最佳答案。

主题：人与自然——自然科学 语篇类型：说明文 词数 263 难度 ★★★★☆ 3 题

①At 4:00 p.m., you are watching a football game live from Europe. At the same time, your friend in New York is sleeping—it's 4:00 a.m. there! Why is the time so different? Why do people in other places start their day when we are already in the middle of ours?

②That's because of time zones. Even though we live on the same planet, people in different places are doing different things at the same time.

③How do time zones work? The earth is like a big spinning ball. It turns from west to east. That's why the sun is coming up in the east first. The world is divided into 24 time zones. Every time zone is one hour different from the next.

④Let's look at some examples. In China, people in the East 8 time zone are getting up early and going to school or work. In London, people are enjoying afternoon tea at around 4 p.m. In New York, many people have just finished lunch at that time.

⑤Time zones also bring some problems to our modern life. For example, a video meeting may start at 10 a.m. in Beijing, but it's midnight in New York. These time differences make it important to plan carefully and respect others' time.

⑥Time zones don't just change the clock—they change our daily life. Time makes us different—but it also makes the world colorful and fun.

📖 参考译文（点击展开）

①下午 4 点，你正在观看一场来自欧洲的足球赛现场直播。与此同时，你在纽约的朋友正在睡觉——那里是凌晨 4 点！为什么时间差别这么大？为什么别处的人开始一天的生活时，我们已经过到一半了？

②那是因为时区。尽管我们生活在同一个星球上，不同地方的人在同一时刻做的事情却各不相同。

③时区是怎么运作的？地球就像一个巨大的旋转球。它自西向东转。那就是为什么太阳先从东方升起。世界被分成 24 个时区。每个时区比相邻的时区早或晚一小时。

④我们来看一些例子。在中国，处于东八区的人们早早起床去上学或上班。在伦敦，人们大约下午 4 点正在享用下午茶。在纽约，那时许多人刚吃完午饭。

⑤时区也给我们的现代生活带来一些问题。比如，一场视频会议可能在北京上午 10 点开始，但在纽约却是午夜。这些时差让仔细规划、尊重他人的时间变得很重要。

⑥时区改变的不仅是时钟——它们改变我们的日常生活。时间让我们彼此不同——但它也让这个世界多姿多彩、充满乐趣。
