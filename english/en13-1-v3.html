Passage C · 记叙文 · 人物与社会Min Sun
闵子骞单衣顺母

词数 135
难度 ★★★☆☆
建议用时 7′
生词 26 条
英文原文 · 点击横线词汇查看释义

Min Sun was a man in the State of Lu. He was a student of Confucius. Among all students of Confucius, he was famous for moral conduct(德行).

Min Sun's mother passed away very early. Later his father took another wife. She gave birth to two sons. The stepmother wasn't good to Min Sun. In winter, two younger brothers wore winter clothes made of cotton, but Min Sun only wore clothes with reed catkins(芦苇絮).

One day, Min Sun followed his father to go out. He accidentally(偶然地) dropped something onto the ground. Then his father hit him. The reed catkins flew out from the broken seam(接缝) of the thin clothes, and then his father knew that his wife wasn't good to Min Sun.

After the father came back home, he wanted his wife to leave his home. Min Sun fell to his knees and asked his father to forgive(原谅) the stepmother. He said, "If Mother stays at home, only I myself have to feel cold. But if you drive Mother away, all three children will have to feel cold."

His father was deeply moved and took Min Sun's advice. The stepmother heard of this, felt sorry and began to be good to Min Sun. And she treated Min Sun as her own son.

01

“Min Sun was a man in the State of Lu … he was famous for moral conduct.”

【译文】闵子骞是鲁国人，是孔子的学生。在孔子所有弟子中，他以德行著称。

  • 开篇定位：一句话交代国籍（State of Lu）、身份（student of Confucius）、特点（famous for moral conduct）。
  • 语言点：be famous for 后接出名的原因；moral conduct 意为"德行、品行"。
  • 解题关联：第 1 题的 ①③ 均出自本段，②④ 为无中生有，故答案为 C。
02

“The stepmother wasn't good to Min Sun. In winter, two younger brothers wore winter clothes made of cotton, but Min Sun only wore clothes with reed catkins.”

【译文】母亲早逝，父亲续弦，继母又生了两个儿子。继母对闵子骞不好：冬天弟弟们穿棉花做的冬衣，他只能穿塞芦苇絮的衣服。

  • 叙事功能：交代矛盾起因，为下文"芦苇絮飞出"埋下伏笔。
  • 语言点：pass away = die（委婉语）；take another wife 续弦；be made of 由……制成（看得出原材料）。
  • 对比手法：cotton 与 reed catkins 形成鲜明对比，突出待遇之别。
03

“The reed catkins flew out from the broken seam of the thin clothes, and then his father knew that his wife wasn't good to Min Sun.”

【译文】一天，闵子骞随父出行，不小心把东西掉在地上，父亲打了他；芦苇絮从单衣破了的接缝里飞出，父亲这才知情。

  • 关键细节：芦苇絮从衣缝飞出，是父亲知情的唯一线索——第 2 题选 C 的直接依据。
  • 语言点：accidentally 偶然地（= by accident）；that his wife wasn't good to Min Sun 为宾语从句。
04

“If Mother stays at home, only I myself have to feel cold. But if you drive Mother away, all three children will have to feel cold.”

【译文】父亲想赶走妻子，闵子骞跪下求情："母在一子寒，母去三子单。"

  • 人物刻画：以德报怨、为弟弟着想——第 3 题选 A 的原因（B、C 与原文相反）。
  • 语言点：fall to one's knees 跪下；drive … away 赶走；两个 if 引导条件状语从句，用一般现在时表将来。
  • 写作手法：直接引语使情感更真切，是记叙文点题之笔。
05

“His father was deeply moved and took Min Sun's advice … she treated Min Sun as her own son.”

【译文】父亲深受感动并采纳建议；继母听说后心生愧疚，从此善待闵子骞，视如己出。

  • 结局：矛盾化解，呼应开头的"moral conduct"，人物形象完整。
  • 语言点：take one's advice 采纳建议；hear of 听说；treat … as … 把……当作……。
  • 解题关联：第 4 题人物特征题，全文突出其善良宽容，选 B. Kind。

01(新考法 · 细节组合题) What do we know about Min Sun?

A. ①②
B. ③④
C. ①③
D. ①④
解析：①由 "He was a student of Confucius" 可知正确；③由 "a man in the State of Lu" 可知正确；②"成为名师"、④"对兄弟不友好"文中均未提及。故选 C。

02How did Min Sun's father know that his wife wasn't good to Min Sun?

A. He found that Min Sun had little to eat.
B. He saw that Min Sun was in old clothes.
C. He saw that the reed catkins flew out from the clothes.
解析：第三段明确写到芦苇絮从破缝飞出后 "his father knew that his wife wasn't good to Min Sun"。A、B 均非文中依据。故选 C。

03Why did Min Sun want his stepmother to stay at home?

A. Because he loved his brothers.
B. Because his stepmother was kind.
C. Because his stepmother was good at housework.
解析：他说"若母亲留下，只我一人受冻；若赶走母亲，三个孩子都要受冻"，可见是为弟弟们着想。B 与原文 "wasn't good to Min Sun" 矛盾；C 无依据。故选 A。

04(新考法 · 推断人物特征题) Which is the best word to describe Min Sun?

A. Strong.
B. Kind.
C. Smart.
解析：综合全文，他受虐仍为继母求情、为弟弟着想，最突出的品质是"善良"。故选 B。

Passage D · 说明文 · 人与社会The Magic of
World Book Day

词数 168
难度 ★★★☆☆
建议用时 7′
生词 24 条
英文原文 · 点击横线词汇查看释义

23 April is World Book and Copyright Day. It was set up by UNESCO in 1995 to encourage people around the world to enjoy reading and respect the creators of books.

This special day was chosen to honour some of the world's most famous writers, like William Shakespeare and Miguel de Cervantes, who both passed away on this day. It also connects to a beautiful tradition from Catalonia, Spain, where a brave knight(骑士) named George received a book as a gift, symbolizing courage and wisdom. Today, this tradition has become a custom where people exchange books and roses.

On World Book Day, many schools and libraries hold fun activities to promote(促进) reading. These can include reading marathons, book fairs, and storytelling events. It's a day to remind us that books are like keys that open doors to new worlds. Through books, we can learn about animals, science, history, and different cultures.

01

“23 April is World Book and Copyright Day. It was set up by UNESCO in 1995 …”

【译文】4 月 23 日是世界图书与版权日，由联合国教科文组织于 1995 年设立，旨在鼓励全世界的人享受阅读、尊重书籍创作者。

  • 信息点：时间（23 April）、设立机构（UNESCO）、设立年份（1995）、设立目的（to encourage … and respect …）。
  • 语言点：be set up 被设立；encourage sb. to do sth.；to + 动词原形 表目的。
  • 解题关联：第 1 题由 "in 1995" 与 "23 April" 组合得 A（23 April, 1995）。
02

“This special day was chosen to honour some of the world's most famous writers … who both passed away on this day.”

【译文】这一天选来纪念莎士比亚、塞万提斯等著名作家，他们都于这一天逝世；它还与西班牙加泰罗尼亚的美丽传统有关：骑士乔治获赠一本书，象征勇气与智慧；如今人们在这天互赠书籍与玫瑰。

  • 语言点：honour 纪念/致敬；who both passed away on this day 为非限制性定语从句；symbolizing … 现在分词作伴随状语。
  • 文化背景：加泰罗尼亚"圣乔治节"传统——互赠书与玫瑰。
  • 解题关联：第 2 题问日期由来，文中给出的理由是"几位著名作家的忌日"，故选 B。
03

“These can include reading marathons, book fairs, and storytelling events. It's a day to remind us that books are like keys that open doors to new worlds.”

【译文】许多学校和图书馆举办活动推广阅读，包括阅读马拉松、书展和讲故事活动。这一天提醒我们：书就像钥匙，为我们打开通往新世界的大门。

  • 语言点：promote reading 推广阅读；remind sb. that + 句子；books are like keys 明喻，that open doors to new worlds 为定语从句修饰 keys。
  • 段落作用：由"活动"上升到"书籍的意义"，点明 The Magic of 的含义。
  • 解题关联：第 3 题 A、B 内容文中均有，故选 C；第 4 题主旨为介绍读书日的历史与活动，选 A。

01When was World Book and Copyright Day set up?

A. 23 April, 1995.
B. 7 October, 1962.
C. 23 April, 1930.
解析：首段 "It was set up by UNESCO in 1995"，节日本身定于 4 月 23 日。故选 A。

02Why was 23 April chosen as the date for World Book Day?

A. It is the birthday of William Shakespeare.
B. It is the anniversary of the death of several famous writers.
C. It is the birthday of Vicente Clavel Andrés.
解析：原文 "… famous writers … who both passed away on this day"，即几位名家的忌日。A 与原文相反，C 文中未提及。故选 B。

03What do people do on World Book Day?

A. They hold reading marathons and book fairs.
B. They exchange books and roses.
C. Both A and B.
解析：A 见第三段，B 见第二段结尾，两者均有。故选 C。

04What is the main idea of the passage?

A. To explain the history and activities of World Book Day.
B. To talk about the importance of copyright.
C. To describe a book fair in Spain.
解析：全文按"由来—日期缘由—节日活动与意义"展开，B、C 均为细节或无中生有。故选 A。

还原阅读六选五 · 人物传记Yang Chen-Ning
— A Great Scientist

词数 227
难度 ★★★★☆
建议用时 7′
生词 26 条
英文原文 · 点击横线词汇查看释义

Yang Chen-Ning was a world-famous physicist and a Nobel Prize winner. He passed away in Beijing on 18 October, 2025, when he was 103 years old. As a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and a teacher at Tsinghua University, 1. ______ because of his great contributions.

Yang was born in Hefei, Anhui in 1922. He had a wonderful study life. First, he studied at famous universities in China. Later, 2. ______, and got his PhD from the University of Chicago. In 1957, he worked with his partner Tsung-Dao Lee and put forward the theory of parity non-conservation in weak interaction. 3. ______, they won the Nobel Prize in Physics. Yang's "Yang-Mills Theory" is also seen as an important part of modern physics.

Yang loved his motherland deeply. In 1971, he returned to China for the first time since 1945. After that, he helped build links between Chinese and American scientists. He gave advice on China's scientific development, raised money for Chinese scholars to study abroad, and in 1999, 4. ______. He worked hard to train young talent and develop basic scientific research in China.

Yang Chen-Ning lived a long life of 103 years. He was a legend who explored the unknown and served his country. 5. ______ will always encourage young people to work hard for science and national development.

01

“As a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and a teacher at Tsinghua University, 1. ______ because of his great contributions.”

【译文】杨振宁是世界著名的物理学家、诺贝尔奖得主。他于 2025 年 10 月 18 日在北京逝世，享年 103 岁。作为中国科学院院士和清华大学教师，他因其巨大贡献而被誉为"不朽"。

  • 解题关键：空后为原因状语 because of his great contributions，需填含谓语的完整结构；F "he was hailed as 'immortal' in the university's notice" 语义、语法均契合。
  • 语言点：a world-famous physicist；be hailed as 被誉为；contribution 常用复数。
02

“Later, 2. ______, and got his PhD from the University of Chicago … 3. ______, they won the Nobel Prize in Physics.”

【译文】他 1922 年生于安徽合肥：先就读于中国著名大学，后赴美深造，在芝加哥大学获得博士学位。1957 年与李政道合作提出弱相互作用中宇称不守恒理论，正因为这项工作，他们获得诺贝尔物理学奖。

  • 2 空：与 and got his PhD 构成并列谓语，指赴美求学 → B。
  • 3 空：作原因状语，逗号后接结果 → A "For this great work"。
  • 语言点：get one's PhD；put forward 提出；be seen as 被视为。
03

“He gave advice on China's scientific development, raised money for Chinese scholars to study abroad, and in 1999, 4. ______.”

【译文】他深爱祖国，1971 年首次回国，此后帮助建立中美科学家之间的联系，为中国科学发展建言献策、为中国学者出国留学筹款，并于 1999 年回到清华大学任教，努力培养青年人才、发展基础科学研究。

  • 解题关键：in 1999 时间状语后需接谓语，D "he went back to Tsinghua University" 与首段"清华大学教师"呼应。
  • 语言点：build links between A and B；raise money for；study abroad（abroad 前不加介词）。
04

“He was a legend who explored the unknown and served his country. 5. ______ will always encourage young people …”

【译文】杨振宁享年 103 岁，是探索未知、报效祖国的传奇人物。他追求真理、热爱祖国的精神将永远激励青年为科学与国家发展而奋斗。

  • 解题关键：空处作主语，需名词短语 → E "His spirit of seeking truth and loving his motherland"。
  • 语言点：explore the unknown（the + adj. 表一类事物）；serve one's country 报效祖国。
  • 多余项：C（He made great progress in his study）与各空语境均不符。

01–05还原阅读答案

1 — F   he was hailed as "immortal" in the university's notice
2 — B   he went to the US to learn more
3 — A   For this great work
4 — D   he went back to Tsinghua University
5 — E   His spirit of seeking truth and loving his motherland
解题总纲：先看空格在句中的成分（1、2、4 需谓语；3 需原因状语；5 需主语名词短语），再看前后逻辑呼应（"teacher at Tsinghua University" ↔ "went back to Tsinghua University"）。多余项为 C。

集有所用（1–6）

  • 01a world-famous physicist 一位世界著名的物理学家
  • 02the Chinese Academy of Sciences 中国科学院
  • 03be hailed as... 被誉为……
  • 04great contributions 巨大贡献
  • 05go to the US to learn more 去美国深造
  • 06get one's PhD 获得博士学位

集有所用（7–12）

  • 07put forward 提出
  • 08build links between A and B 在 A 和 B 之间建立联系
  • 09train young talent 培养青年人才
  • 10develop basic scientific research 发展基础科学研究
  • 11serve one's country 报效祖国
  • 12the spirit of seeking truth 追求真理的精神

识有所悟 · 诺贝尔奖

  • ★诺贝尔奖是以瑞典化学家诺贝尔的遗产设立的国际奖项，旨在表彰对人类做出杰出贡献的人。
  • ★最初设有物理学、化学、生理学或医学、文学与和平五个奖项，后增设经济学奖。
  • ★每年评选一次，获奖者来自世界各地，代表相关领域的最高成就，是全球公认的最高荣誉之一。
