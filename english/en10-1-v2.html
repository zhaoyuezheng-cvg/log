一、阅读理解（Passage 1）

主题：人与自然——自然环境　语篇类型：应用文　词数：163　难度：★★★☆☆　建议用时：5′　正确率：B

阅读下面短文，从每题 A、B、C 三个选项中选出一个能回答所提问题或完成所给句子的最佳答案。

提示：点击文中带虚线的英文生词或短语，可查看中文翻译、用法解析与例句。

Most studies show that many animals can feel weather changes. Here are some interesting things about animals.

Animals Behaviours(行为方式) Weather changes
Birds A lot of birds get together and stay under the roofs(屋檐) of houses. Heavy rain will come.
Sharks A lot of birds get together and stay under the roofs(屋檐) of houses. A big storm will arrive.
Spiders There are more spiders than usual in autumn, and the spiders are making larger webs(网) than usual. A cold and dry winter will come.
Ants A lot of ants are on the grass. It is going to rain.

校对说明：原表 Sharks 一行的“行为方式”与 Birds 行文字重复，疑似原文排版重复；此处按原文档保留，不影响本题作答。

The world of animals is really amazing. Sometimes you can not explain(解释) why they can tell the weather before the weather report. If you want to know more about them, you can read more articles about science in School Science Magazine.

  1. If spiders are getting more and they are making larger webs than usual in autumn, the weather in the following winter will be
    A. wet and warmB. dry and coldC. wet and cold
  2. What can we learn from the passage?
    A. In winter, there are no spiders making webs.B. If too many birds or ants get together, it will rain.C. Scientists can't explain the behaviours of animals.
  3. （新考法·文章出处题）Where can we read the passage?
    A. In a storybook.B. In a travel guide.C. In a science magazine.

读果小

☆ 读有所思：Can you think of other examples of animals' behaviours about weather changes?

☆ 集有所用：

1. 文章中的比较级单词及其原形：
deeper—deep　more—many　larger—large　fewer—few　smaller—small

2. It is going to rain. 天要下雨了。（be going to 表示根据现象主观判断）

☆ 识有所悟：人们从蜘蛛丝的高强度与韧性中得到启发，研发出防弹衣、医用缝合线等坚韧材料；模仿鲨鱼皮肤的特殊结构，制造出能减少水阻的高速泳衣与船体设计。此外，蝙蝠的回声定位原理还被用于发明雷达和声呐，这些都是经典的仿生学成果。

二、阅读理解（Passage 2）

主题：人与社会——体育健康　语篇类型：记叙文　词数：145　难度：★★★☆☆　建议用时：6′　正确率：4

It's December and it is about -10°C in Harbin these days. Many people think it's too cold, but Zhang Dewu is excited about the cold days.

Zhang Dewu lives in Harbin, Heilongjiang. He is seventy-four years old, but he looks young. Zhang loves winter swimming very much. It's snowing today and it is very cold. But look at the picture! Zhang is swimming in the Songhua River and he is having fun! Before he gets into the cold river, Zhang usually does some exercise like running to make himself warm. The cold and strong winds on the river can't make him afraid.

"Winter swimming helps me to be healthy and I never catch a cold. It makes me happy and I want to keep doing it. But this exercise isn't right for everyone. People with some health problems can't do it," Zhang says.

  1. The weather is ________ in Harbin in December.
    A. warmB. coldC. rainy
  2. What does Zhang usually do before getting into the river?
    A. He takes a shower.B. He has a good sleep.C. He does some running.
  3. The underlined word "it" in the last paragraph refers to ________.
    A. winter exerciseB. winter swimmingC. healthy food
  4. What can we learn from the passage?
    A. Zhang doesn't like snowy days.B. Zhang is good at all kinds of sports.C. Winter swimming is not right for everyone.

三、阅读理解（Passage 3）

主题：人与自我——自我提升　语篇类型：记叙文　词数：212　难度：★★★★☆　建议用时：6′　正确率：/4

Five years ago, on my way home on the bus every night, I always found an old man playing his saxophone(萨克斯) near the river. I could see the big smile on his face. The beautiful music made me dream(梦想着) to play the saxophone just like that man.

Two weeks later, it was my birthday. I opened my present and found that it was a saxophone! I put it to my mouth to play, but it wouldn't make a sound. I blew(吹) and blew but I couldn't get the saxophone to make a sound. I was about to cry when suddenly the saxophone made a sound. I blew again, I moved my fingers on the saxophone and I made more sounds. Some of them were loud(大声的) and clear but many of them weren't. I was happy because I was playing very interesting sounds. But it wasn't beautiful music.

Then came the lesson. Night after night, week after week, I practised hard playing the saxophone. Slowly I started to get better and at last there came the magic day when the beautiful music came out of my saxophone.

Now I'm in the TV studio(电视台) with my saxophone. I'm so happy just like the old man near the river.

  1. What made the writer want to play the saxophone?
    A. The big smile on the old man's face.B. The beautiful music from the old man.C. The birthday present from his parents.
  2. Why is the writer happy in the last paragraph?
    A. Because he meets the old man near the river.B. Because he plays the saxophone well after practice.C. Because he gets a magic saxophone from the studio.
  3. （新考法·情感态度题）Which of the following shows the writer's feelings?
  4. What lesson does the writer learn?
    A. No pain, no gain.B. It's never too old to learn.C. Learning without thinking is of no use.

逐篇详细解析

阅读一 逐段解析与答题依据

第一段：Most studies show that many animals can feel weather changes. 本段点明主旨：许多动物能感知天气变化，引出下文的动物行为。关键词是 weather changes。

表格段：文章用表格列举动物行为与天气变化：Birds 聚集在屋檐下→大雨；Sharks→大风暴；Spiders 秋天更多、织更大的网→寒冷干燥的冬天；Ants 大量出现在草地上→要下雨。

  • 第1题：定位 Spiders 一行：There are more spiders than usual in autumn, and the spiders are making larger webs than usual. 对应天气是 A cold and dry winter will come. 因此选 B. dry and cold。
  • 第2题：A 项“冬天没有蜘蛛织网”文章未说，且过于绝对；B 项“太多鸟或蚂蚁聚集，会下雨”与 Birds 行和 Ants 行相符；C 项“科学家不能解释动物行为”偷换概念，原文是“有时你无法解释”，并非“科学家不能解释所有动物行为”。故选 B。
  • 第3题：文章末尾提到 School Science Magazine，并建议读更多 science articles，因此最可能出现在科学杂志上，选 C。

读果小：比较级原形：deeper—deep，more—many，larger—large，fewer—few，smaller—small。It is going to rain. 中 be going to 表示根据现象作出的主观判断。

识有所悟：蜘蛛丝、鲨鱼皮肤、蝙蝠回声定位等启发了仿生学发明，说明动物与科技联系密切。

阅读二 逐段解析与答题依据

第一段：It's December and it is about -10°C in Harbin these days. 哈尔滨十二月约零下10摄氏度，很多人觉得太冷，但张德武对寒冷日子感到兴奋。第1题问天气，选 B. cold。

第二段：张德武74岁但看起来年轻，非常喜欢冬泳。下雪天他在松花江游泳。入水前，他通常做跑步等运动来让自己暖和；冷风和强风不能让他害怕。第2题问入水前做什么，选 C. He does some running.。短语 make himself warm 是“make + 宾语 + 形容词”结构；make him afraid 中 afraid 作宾补。

第三段：冬泳帮助他健康，他从不感冒；冬泳让他快乐，他想继续做；但这项运动不适合所有人，有健康问题的人不能做。第3题 it 指代前文核心活动 winter swimming，选 B。第4题由“this exercise isn't right for everyone”可知选 C。

积累小传答案：1. winter swimming　2. 做运动/锻炼　3. catch a cold　4. health problems　5. excited　6. right for。

句子解析：5. The news made him excited. 中 make sb + adj. 表示“使某人……”。6. I think being a driver is just right for him. 中 be right for sb 表示“适合某人”。

阅读三 逐段解析与答题依据

第一段：五年前，作者每晚坐公交回家，总看到一位老人在河边吹萨克斯。老人脸上的笑容和美妙音乐让作者梦想像他一样吹萨克斯。第1题问什么让作者想吹萨克斯，直接原因是 the beautiful music，选 B。A 项笑容是画面细节，C 项礼物是后文事件。

第二段：两周后生日，作者收到萨克斯礼物。起初吹不响，作者着急得快要哭；突然萨克斯发声，作者又吹并移动手指，发出更多声音。有些声音响亮清晰，但很多不是；作者开心，因为声音有趣，但还不是美妙音乐。这里情感有期待、着急、开心等波动。

第三段：作者夜复一夜、周复一周刻苦练习，慢慢变好，最后美妙音乐从萨克斯中传出。第2题问最后一段作者为什么开心，是因为练习后能吹好萨克斯，选 B。

第四段：现在作者带着萨克斯在电视台演播室，像河边老人一样开心。

第3题情感态度：五年前被音乐吸引，两周后收到礼物开心但吹不响着急，后来能发声小开心，现在在电视台演奏很开心。整体上升，但中间有波动，因此选 B。

第4题主旨：文章强调刻苦练习后才获得美妙音乐，对应 A. No pain, no gain.（不劳无获）。B 项“活到老学到老”和 C 项“学而不思则罔”均不符合文章重点。
