一、阅读理解 D（说明文）

主题：人与自然——自然科学 | 语篇类型：说明文 | 词数：229 | 难度：★★★☆☆ | 建议用时：7'

If you fly across several time zones, you may have jet lag(时差反应). You arrive in a new time zone, but your body is still living on the time in the old time zone. You may be ready for dinner at midnight, and you may want to sleep all day.

People have jet lag because all living things have a biological clock(生物钟). For example, plants begin to work at sunrise. When winter comes, birds fly to warm places. Some insects like bees also seem to set their alarm clocks to look for food.

Some scientists did an experiment(实验) with bees in Paris. They put out sugar water at 4:00 p.m. every day, and the bees came to drink the water at exactly the right time. Then the scientists put bees in a dark room. The bees still came out at the same time. Later the scientists took the bees to New York. The bees came for the food at the right time again, when it was only 10:00 a.m. in New York. Their bodies were still on Paris time.

Humans(人类) have a biological clock too. It tells us when to sleep and eat. Our bodies are controlled by a biological clock, but we can learn to adjust(调整) it to different times.

题目 · 答案 · 解析

( C ) 1. What is the passage about?
A. Time zones.
B. An experiment.
C. Biological clocks.
答案：C
解析：全文围绕“生物钟”展开——第1段由时差反应引出话题，第2段提出“一切生物都有生物钟”，第3段用蜜蜂实验验证，第4段回到人类的生物钟。time zones 只是引出话题的细节，experiment 只是论据，主旨为 C。
( B ) 2. When did bees come out for food in New York?
A. At 10:00 a.m.(Paris time).
B. At 4:00 p.m.(Paris time).
C. At 10:00 p.m.(New York time).
答案：B
解析：原文说蜜蜂到纽约后“came for the food at the right time again, when it was only 10:00 a.m. in New York. Their bodies were still on Paris time”，即纽约上午10点＝巴黎下午4点（两地相差6小时），蜜蜂仍按巴黎的生物钟出行。A 把时区标错，C 把 a.m. 错成 p.m.，故选 B。
( B ) 3.（新考法·写作意图题）What is the purpose(目的) of showing the experiment with bees?
A. To explain how bees set their alarm clocks.
B. To prove that living things have a biological clock.
C. To show that living things can reset their biological clock.
答案：B
解析：实验段（第3段）紧承第2段观点“all living things have a biological clock”。实验结果——蜜蜂在暗室里、被带到纽约后仍按巴黎时间出来取食——正是为了证明生物体内存在生物钟。C 与实验结果相反（蜜蜂并没有重设生物钟），A 只是表面细节，故选 B。
( A ) 4. What can we learn from the passage?
A. Humans can adjust their biological clock.
B. Bees couldn't tell the time in the dark room.
C. Some people have jet lag because they are weak(弱的).
答案：A
解析：末句“we can learn to adjust(调整) it to different times”直接对应 A。B 与原文矛盾（蜜蜂在暗室里仍准时出来）；C 文中无依据（时差源于生物钟而非体弱）。

积累小筏 · 参考答案

  • 1. fly across → 飞越；飞过
  • 2. 做好准备 → get ready for / be ready for
  • 3. at midnight → 在午夜
  • 4. at sunrise → 在日出时
  • 5. 同时 → at the same time
  • 6. when to sleep → 什么时候睡觉
  • 7. are controlled by → 被……控制（被动语态：be + 过去分词 + by）
  • 8. 仿写（我妈妈每周日带我去公园。）→ My mother takes me to the park every Sunday.（take...to... 把……带到……；主语第三人称单数，take 加 s）

二、阅读还原六选五（记叙文）

主题：人与社会——公益事业 | 语篇类型：记叙文 | 词数：216 | 难度：★★★☆☆ | 建议用时：6'

It is a summer afternoon. Sam is walking home from school. He is very happy because he buys a new book with all his money.

1. C — On the way, a man stops Sam. The man is blind. He can't see anything. He says to Sam, “Please give me some money. I am very hungry. 2. A — I want to buy some bread.” Sam is sad because he has no money.

At that moment, a carriage(马车) comes over. 3. B — A boy and his mother sit in it. The boy's name is Harry. The blind man stops in front of the carriage. Harry says to his mother, “Let's give the man something.”

Harry's mother gives Harry some coins(硬币). 4. F — Harry takes them, but he doesn't give the coins to the man. He throws them into the grass by the road. The blind man can't find them because he can't see.

Sam sees that. He starts looking for the coins for the blind man. 5. E — It takes him a lot of time, but he finds them all.

Which of the two boys do you think is truly kind to the blind man?

选项
A. I want to buy some bread.
B. A boy and his mother sit in it.
C. On the way, a man stops Sam.
D. They give the man some food and money.
E. It takes him a lot of time, but he finds them all.
F. Harry takes them, but he doesn't give the coins to the man.
答案：1-5 C A B F E（多余选项：D）
逐题解析
1. C — 空后“The man is blind.”用定冠词 The 首次特指“这个男人”，说明前文必须先引入一个人；C“在路上，一个男人拦住了 Sam”正好引出人物，又承接上文 Sam 放学走路回家。
2. A — 空格在盲人的话里，前句说“I am very hungry”，A“我想买些面包”说明要钱的用途，语意连贯，且人称 I 与引语一致。
3. B — 前句“a carriage comes over”出现马车，B 中 in it 的 it 指代马车，并引入车上的男孩和母亲；后句“The boy's name is Harry.”与 The boy 衔接。
4. F — 前句妈妈把硬币给了 Harry，后句“He throws them into the grass”说明硬币没到盲人手里；F“Harry 接过硬币，但没有把硬币给那个人”承上启下，them 指 coins。
5. E — 前句 Sam 开始为盲人找硬币，E“花了他很多时间，但他全部找到了”交代寻找的结果，him 指 Sam；并与文末“哪个男孩才是真正善良”的设问呼应。
多余项 D“他们给了那个人一些食物和钱”与情节（Harry 扔硬币、Sam 找硬币）不符，故不选。

积累小筏 · 参考答案

  • 1. walk home → 步行回家
  • 2. 在那时 → at that moment / at that time
  • 3. look for → 寻找
  • 4. be kind to → 对……友好／善待……
  • 5. 汽车刚才撞到了路边的一棵树。The car hit a tree by the road / by the roadside just now.
  • 6. 从成都到西昌坐高铁需要花费我们大约三个小时。It takes us about three hours from Chengdu to Xichang on a high-speed train.（It takes sb. + 时间 + to do / 从句：做某事花费某人多长时间）

三、完形填空（记叙文）

主题：人与自我——家庭生活 | 语篇类型：记叙文 | 词数：214 | 难度：★★★☆☆ | 建议用时：7'

It is 8:30 in the evening. I can't 1 study in my room and I keep thinking about my mother's trip to Shanghai. 2 So I close my books and walk out of my room. My mother usually does the dishes, but today my father is doing that in the 3 kitchen now. What about my mother? She is packing(打包) her clothes for her trip. I go to her and ask, “4 When are you leaving home tomorrow, Mum?”

“At 6:30 a.m. I'll come back in three days.”

“I see. How I wish you could take 5 me!”

“Oh, my dear girl, I'll go there to work, not for fun.”

“But you know, it is my favourite 6 place.”

“Don't be sad about it. Our family can go there when your brother gets older.”

“Sounds great. But I'll 7 miss you, Mum.”

“Me too. I'm free at night, and we can make video calls.”

“OK. Can you bring back any gifts from Shanghai?”

“Sure. I can do some shopping in my free time.”

Then I look at my two-year-old brother. He is 9 still playing with his model cars on the bed. He looks happy. He is 10 young, so he doesn't know what we are talking about. I wish him to grow up quickly.

题目 · 答案 · 解析

( A ) 1. A. study B. sleep C. play
答案：A
解析：下句“I close my books”说明“我”原本在房间里学习；因为一直想着妈妈的上海之行而学不进去。sleep、play 与“合上书”的线索不符。
( A ) 2. A. So B. But C. Or
答案：A
解析：前句是原因（学不进去、不停想），后句是结果（合上书走出房间），表因果用 So。
( C ) 3. A. bookcase B. classroom C. kitchen
答案：C
解析：do the dishes（洗碗）发生在厨房 kitchen；bookcase 书柜、classroom 教室均不合常理。
( B ) 4. A. How B. When C. Why
答案：B
解析：答语“At 6:30 a.m.”是时间点，故问“什么时候出发”，用 When。
( B ) 5. A. it B. me C. her
答案：B
解析：说话的是女儿，她希望妈妈“带上我”，take 后接宾格 me。下文妈妈说“我是去工作不是玩”正是对这一请求的回应。
( A ) 6. A. place B. sport C. bridge
答案：A
解析：上海是“我最喜爱的地方”place；sport 运动、bridge 桥与语境不符。
( B ) 7. A. help B. miss C. join
答案：B
解析：妈妈答“Me too... we can make video calls”（我也想您，可以视频通话），说明双方互相思念，选 miss。
( C ) 8. A. see B. show C. bring
答案：C
解析：bring back 带回；“你能从上海带些礼物回来吗？”see back、show back 均无此搭配。
( A ) 9. A. still B. also C. either
答案：A
解析：弟弟“仍然”在床上玩模型车，用 still。also 用于肯定句句中表“也”，either 用于否定句，均不合句意。
( C ) 10. A. cute B. smart C. young
答案：C
解析：so 后是结果“他不知道我们在谈什么”，原因应是他才两岁、年纪小 young；cute 可爱、smart 聪明与因果逻辑矛盾。

积累小筏 · 参考答案

  • 1. think about → 思考；考虑
  • 2. 从……走出 → walk out of...
  • 3. come back → 回来；返回
  • 4. 在晚上 → at night / in the evening
  • 5. 下个月去上海迪士尼公园怎么样？What / How about going to Shanghai Disneyland Park next month?（What/How about + doing 提建议）
  • 6. 我住在超市旁边。对我来说，买东西非常便利。It's very convenient for me to do some shopping / do the shopping.
