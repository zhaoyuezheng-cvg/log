A

电影《龙猫》 My Neighbour Totoro

P1 · 一、阅读理解 A
主题：人与社会——影视概况 ｜ 语篇类型：应用文 ｜ 词数：110 ｜ 难度： ｜ 建议用时：6′ ｜ 正确率：__/3

Movie name: My Neighbour Totoro

Year: 1988　Time: 86 minutes

Story: Two young girls, Satsuki and Mei, go to a new place with their father. Their mother is ill in hospital near it. One day, Mei finds Totoro and only kind children can see him. Totoro looks kind of big and terrible, but he always helps them when they are in trouble.

① Mei finds Totoro and she is sleeping on Totoro.
② Satsuki and Mei are standing with Totoro in the rain.
③ Satsuki and Mei are taking Totoro's bus to see their mother.
④ Totoro is taking Satsuki and Mei flying through the night sky.

1.Totoro is always ______ Satsuki and Mei.

答案解析
选 A（kind to）。be kind to sb “对某人友好”。由文中 “he always helps them when they are in trouble”（他总是帮助他们）可知龙猫对姐妹俩很友善。be strict with “对……严格”；be sorry for “为……感到抱歉”，均与句意不符。

2.新考法 · 图文匹配题In Picture ______, Satsuki and Mei are going to the hospital.

答案解析
选 C（③）。图③文字是 “Satsuki and Mei are taking Totoro's bus to see their mother”（乘坐龙猫巴士去看妈妈），而妈妈正在住院（ill in hospital），所以“去医院”对应图③。图①小梅睡在龙猫身上；图②雨中站立；图④夜空中飞行，都与“去医院”无关。

3.What can we learn from the text?

答案解析
选 C。A 错：文中说 “only kind children can see him”（只有善良的孩子才能看见他），并非任何人；B 错：电影 1988 年上映，距今已三十多年，不是“约二十年”；C 对：图③④告诉我们龙猫带姐妹俩乘巴士看妈妈、在夜空飞行，做了很多有趣的事。

积累小筏 · A篇（P1）

  • 短语互译 1. in hospital → 住院
  • 短语互译 2. 一天 → one day
  • 完成句子 3. 考拉有点意思，但老虎真的很吓人。
    Koalas are ______ ______ interesting but tigers are really scary. 答案：kind of（有点）
  • 完成句子 4. 我父亲总是告诉我，遇到困难时，永远不要放弃。
    My father always tells me never to give up when I am ______ ______. 答案：in trouble（处于困境）
B

文化习俗面面观

P2 · 一、阅读理解 B
主题：人与社会——文化习俗 ｜ 语篇类型：记叙文 ｜ 词数：176 ｜ 难度： ｜ 建议用时：6′ ｜ 正确率：__/4

Calvin: I'm Calvin and I come from New Zealand. Our Christmas is in summer, so we have beach parties under the stars. Christmas here is really different from Christmas in places where it snows.

Maria: I, Maria, come from Australia. People in China are celebrating the Spring Festival and family members are reuniting for a New Year's Eve dinner, while people in Australia may be taking a walk to enjoy the cool summer night or preparing for their New Year celebrations. This gives me a deep understanding of how time zones affect people's daily life.

Huck: I, Huck, am from Spain. Now I'll tell you about siesta time — our special break in the middle of the day. We sit down with family for a relaxing meal, unlike the quick lunches in other places. We usually have lunch and have a nap from 2:00 to 5:00 in the afternoon. And at night, Barcelona really comes to life! The sun sets late, so we hold parties and enjoy ourselves at midnight.

1.How does New Zealand celebrate Christmas according to the passage?

答案解析
选 B（By holding beach parties）。由 Calvin 的话 “Our Christmas is in summer, so we have beach parties under the stars” 可知，新西兰的圣诞节在夏天，人们在海滩举办派对庆祝。A、C 文中未提及。

2.新考法 · 跨学科知识题In which season is New Year in Australia?

答案解析
选 B（Summer）。跨学科知识：澳大利亚位于南半球，季节与北半球相反——北半球冬天时南半球正是夏天；文中 “enjoy the cool summer night” 也印证 1 月的新年时节为夏季。春节在北半球是冬天，但那是中国。

3.If you call Huck at 3:30 p.m. in Spain and he doesn't pick up, what might he be doing?

答案解析
选 A（He is having lunch or sleeping）。Huck 说 “We usually have lunch and have a nap from 2:00 to 5:00 in the afternoon”（我们通常 2 点到 5 点吃午饭并睡午觉），下午 3:30 正在这个时段，所以他可能在吃午饭或睡觉。聚会与晚餐都在晚上，B、C 不符。

4.What is the passage mainly about?

答案解析
选 A。全文通过三位同学介绍新西兰的圣诞海滩派对、澳大利亚的新年时节、西班牙的午休与夜生活，主题是“不同国家的节日与日常习惯”。B 只涉及圣诞节，以偏概全；C 只提西班牙午餐，同样片面。

积累小练 · B篇（P2）

  • 短语互译 1. 来自 → come from
  • 短语互译 2. 与……不同 → be different from
  • 短语互译 3. the Spring Festival → 春节
  • 短语互译 4. New Year's Eve dinner → 年夜饭
  • 短语互译 5. 为……做准备 → prepare for
  • 短语互译 6. have a nap → 小睡一会儿；睡午觉
  • 短语互译 7. come to life → 活跃起来；热闹起来
  • 短语互译 8. 玩得开心 → enjoy oneself（= have fun）
  • 翻译句子 9. This gives me a deep understanding of how time zones affect people's daily life.
    这让我深刻理解到时区如何影响人们的日常生活。 注意 deep understanding of “对……的深刻理解”；how 引导的从句作 of 的宾语。
  • 仿写句子 10. 原句：Christmas here is really different from Christmas in places where it snows.
    仿写（这里的生活和大城市的生活大不相同）：
    Life here is really different from life in big cities. 套用 be different from 结构，把 “Christmas / places where it snows” 换成 “life / big cities”。
C

爱心音乐会

P3 · 一、阅读理解 C
主题：人与社会——公益事业 ｜ 语篇类型：记叙文 ｜ 词数：162 ｜ 难度： ｜ 建议用时：7′ ｜ 正确率：__/4

It is Saturday today. Steven doesn't need to go to school. But he and his classmates still meet at the school gate at 8:00 in the morning. Why? Because they have to go to the City Park.

They take a bus and get to the park at 8:30 a.m. At 9 o'clock, the students are giving a concert. Steven is playing the piano. He does it well. Diana, Steven's deskmate, is singing an English song. Jenny is good at playing the violin, and she is playing it now. David is playing the drums. Many people in the park are listening to their music.

Why do the children give this concert? They do it for Nick, a boy in their class. Nick is ill in hospital. The students want to help him. They give this concert to raise money for him. They hope Nick can get well and come back to school early.

1.Where do the students meet?

答案解析
选 C（At the school gate）。第一段 “he and his classmates still meet at the school gate at 8:00 in the morning”，他们是在校门口集合，不是直接去公园或音乐会现场。

2.新考法 · 图文匹配题What is Steven doing at 9:00 a.m.?

答案解析
选 A（弹钢琴）。第二段 “Steven is playing the piano. He does it well.” 说明上午 9 点 Steven 正在弹钢琴，故选钢琴图。拉小提琴的是 Jenny，唱歌的是 Diana。

3.What does the underlined word "it" in Paragraph 3 refer to?

答案解析
选 C（Giving the concert）。第三段 “They do it for Nick” 中 it 指代上一句提到的动作 “give this concert”（举办这场音乐会）：他们是为了 Nick 才办这场音乐会的。指代题要回看前文找先行内容。

4.新考法 · 推断人物特征题Which word can best describe the students?

答案解析
选 B（Kind）。学生们为住院的同学 Nick 办音乐会筹款、希望他早日康复，说明他们善良、有爱心（kind）。strict“严厉的”、quiet“安静的”都与文意不符。

积累小筏 · C篇（P3）

  • 短语互译 1. take a bus → 乘坐公共汽车
  • 短语互译 2. 弹钢琴 → play the piano
  • 短语互译 3. play the violin → 拉小提琴
  • 短语互译 4. 打鼓 → play the drums
  • 完成句子 5. 为了学好英语，他必须每天都听磁带。
    To learn English well, he ______ ______ ______ tapes every day. 答案：must listen to（必须听）
  • 完成句子 6. 你的同学们是怎样为那位住院的贫困老人筹款的？
    How did your classmates ______ ______ for the poor old man ______ ______? 答案：raise money ... in hospital（筹款……住院）
