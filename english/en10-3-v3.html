Passage C — Polar Bears (北极熊)

主题：人与自然 — 自然生态 语篇类型：说明文 词数：240 难度：★★★☆☆

Polar bears are facing hard times as their icy home goes away. Scientists found that as the Arctic sea ice goes away because of rising temperatures, it is hard for these bears to find their most important food — ringed seals (环斑海豹), which they usually hunt on the ice.

With less ice, polar bears are spending more time on land, trying to eat things like birds' eggs, berries and grass. But this diet isn't good enough for them, and they start to lose weight quickly, which can lead to serious problems.

The things happening in Western Manitoba, an important place for these bears, show just ▲. Here, the time without ice grew by three weeks from 1979 to 2015. To better understand what's happening to the bears, scientists followed 20 of them over three summers to see what they were doing and eating.

What they found wasn't very hopeful. Almost all the bears in the study were trying different ways to keep away from hunger, like resting to save energy or looking for berries, but they still lost weight, some up to 11% of their body weight.

The story of the polar bears is a clear sign of how changes in the earth's weather are affecting our planet.

While hunting once was a big problem for these bears, it's now the warming world that's their biggest problem, changing the very nature of their Arctic home and making it hard for them to live.

( ) 1. What does the underlined word "diet" in Paragraph 2 mean in Chinese?

( ) 2. (新考法 · 信息还原题) Which of the following is the best to be put in "▲" in Paragraph 3?

( ) 3. What did scientists find out about polar bears in the study?

( ) 4. What does the passage mainly tell us?

☑ 参考答案与解析

1. 【B】饮食
解析：根据第二段上下文，北极熊在陆地上尝试吃鸟蛋、浆果和草等东西，但这种"饮食"不足以满足它们的需求，导致它们迅速掉体重。diet 意为"饮食；日常食物"，故选 B。A项"污染"和C项"活动"均不符合语境。
2. 【B】how serious things have become
解析：第三段提到 Manitoba 地区无冰时间从1979年到2015年增加了三周，这是对北极熊生存状况恶化的具体描述，说明情况变得有多严重。前文说"这些情况展示了仅仅是___"，后文用具体数据说明问题的严重性，因此填入"情况变得有多严重"最合适。故选 B。
3. 【A】They all lost weight.
解析：第四段明确指出"Almost all the bears in the study...but they still lost weight"（研究中几乎所有的熊......但它们仍然体重下降），说明科学家发现熊们都体重减轻了。故选 A。B项"都喜欢休息"和C项"都喜欢吃浆果"只是部分行为描述，不是研究发现的核心结论。
4. 【A】Polar bears are facing hunger.
解析：全文围绕北极熊因北极海冰融化而面临食物短缺、体重下降的困境展开。第一段总述北极熊面临困难，第二段讲它们改变饮食但仍体重下降，第三段用 Manitoba 的数据说明问题严重性，第四段讲研究发现熊们都在挨饿，最后两段总结这是气候变化带来的威胁。因此文章主要告诉我们北极熊正面临饥饿。故选 A。

Passage D — Dongming Grain Painting (东明粒画)

主题：人与社会 — 传统文化 语篇类型：说明文 词数：201 难度：★★★☆☆

Do you know that seeds (种子) can be used to make beautiful paintings? Dongming grain painting is a good example.

Dongming grain painting has a history of more than 200 years. In 2016, it was named Shandong's intangible cultural heritage (非物质文化遗产). In the past, people gave grain paintings as important gifts when it was time for the "Three-Animal Sacrifice" (三牲祭祀).

Han Guorui, 44, started learning Dongming grain paintings when he was 8. During these years, he saw the art become less popular. People are losing interest in it, maybe because it takes much time and work to make one. It usually takes 27 steps (步骤) ▲. For example, Han spent five years growing red corn (玉米) for making plum blossom petals (梅花花瓣).

To keep people interested, Han thought of some new ideas. And they are working too. In 2014, he made a grain painting of the Mona Lisa. Hopefully, it sold for much money, and he succeeded in making more people know about Chinese culture!

( ) 1. What do you know about Dongming grain paintings?

( ) 2. (新考法 · 信息还原题) Which sentence can be put in ▲ ?

( ) 3. Why did Han Guorui make a grain painting of the Mona Lisa?

( ) 4. (新考法 · 写作意图题) What's the purpose (目的) of the passage?

☑ 参考答案与解析

1. 【A】People use seeds to make Dongming grain paintings.
解析：第一段明确指出"seeds can be used to make beautiful paintings"（种子可以用来制作美丽的画作），东明粒画就是很好的例子。A项正确。B项文中说过去人们用粒画作礼物，但未说今天仍然如此；C项文中说历史超过200年，而非约100年。故选 A。
2. 【C】and some seeds are difficult to get
解析：前文说"制作一幅粒画通常需要27个步骤"，后文举例"韩国瑞花了五年时间种植红玉米来做梅花花瓣"，说明某些材料（种子）很难获取。C项"有些种子很难弄到"与上下文逻辑一致。A项"需要很多钱"和B项"人们互相帮助"均与后文举例不衔接。故选 C。
3. 【C】To make people interested in grain paintings.
解析：第四段开头说"To keep people interested"（为了保持人们的兴趣），然后提到他画了蒙娜丽莎的粒画，最终"he succeeded in making more people know about Chinese culture"（他成功让更多人了了解中国文化）。因此画蒙娜丽莎的目的是让人们重新对粒画产生兴趣。故选 C。
4. 【B】To tell people about the grain paintings.
解析：全文介绍了东明粒画的历史（200多年）、被命名为非物质文化遗产、制作工艺复杂（27个步骤）、传承人韩国瑞的努力以及创新尝试。文章的主旨是向读者介绍粒画这种传统文化艺术。故选 B。A项"展示不同种类的画"偏离主题；C项"讲述韩国瑞如何学画"只是文中一部分内容，不是全文目的。

✎ 积累小助手

2. lose interest in... → 对......失去兴趣
3. think of new ideas → 想出新主意
4. 成功做某事 → succeed in doing sth.

仿写句子：

Passage C — Seasonal Sayings (季节谚语)

主题：人与社会 — 语言与文化 语篇类型：说明文 词数：245 难度：★★★☆☆

Every season brings special things — flowers in spring, sunshine in summer, colourful leaves in autumn, and snow in winter. There are many fun sayings and expressions about these seasons. Let's get to know some of them!

Spring: A Fresh Start

Spring is all about new beginnings, so people often say, "April showers bring May flowers." This means the rain in April helps flowers bloom (开花) in May. It's a way of telling us that good things often come after a little trouble.

Summer: Fun in the Sun

Summer is hot, sunny, and full of life. A popular saying is "Make hay (草料) while the sun shines." This means to make good use of a good situation (形势) while it lasts. Farmers often say this because they can only dry hay on sunny days.

Autumn: A Time for Change

When leaves turn red, orange and yellow, people often say, "The autumn of one's life". It refers to the later years of a person's life, as autumn is later in the year.

Winter: Comfortable and Cold

Winter can be hard, but people make the best of it. You may hear someone say, "snowed under". It means being very busy, like when snow piles up (堆积) all around you.

Seasonal sayings are like little pieces of wisdom (智慧) in fun words. So next time you hear one, smile and think about how it works in the world around you!

( ) 1. (新考法 · 跨学科知识题) What is the best Chinese meaning for the underlined sentence?

( ) 2. (新考法 · 句意理解题) What can we learn from "Make hay while the sun shines."?

( ) 3. What is the main idea of the passage?

( ) 4. (新考法 · 文章出处题) In which part of a website (网站) can we find the passage?

☑ 参考答案与解析

1. 【C】宝剑锋从磨砺出，梅花香自苦寒来。
解析：文中划线句"April showers bring May flowers"意为"四月的雨水带来五月的花朵"，比喻好事往往在经历困难之后到来。C项"宝剑锋从磨砺出，梅花香自苦寒来"同样表达了"美好事物需要经历磨难才能到来"的含义，最为贴切。A项描写秋景，B项描写四月花尽桃花开，均不符合。故选 C。
2. 【A】It teaches us to work when having a chance.
解析：文中明确解释"Make hay while the sun shines"的意思是"to make good use of a good situation while it lasts"（在好形势还在时好好利用），即"趁有机会时努力工作"。故选 A。B项"晴天休息"和C项"春天晒草"均曲解了谚语含义。
3. 【C】Seasonal sayings help us understand life and nature.
解析：文章分别介绍了春夏秋冬四个季节的谚语，并解释了每个谚语背后的含义和哲理。最后一段总结"Seasonal sayings are like little pieces of wisdom in fun words"（季节谚语就像趣味文字中的小小智慧），说明季节谚语帮助我们理解生活和自然。故选 C。A项"享受所有季节"和B项"不同季节有不同天气"都不是文章主旨。
4. 【C】Nature.
解析：文章主题是四季谚语与自然的关系，涉及春天的花、夏天的阳光、秋天的落叶和冬天的雪，属于自然类话题。文章出处应在网站的自然（Nature）版块。故选 C。A项"健康"和B项"故事"与文章主题不符。

Passage D — Why Are We Sleepy on Rainy Days? (雨天为什么困？)

主题：人与自然 — 人类活动 语篇类型：说明文 词数：289 难度：★☆☆☆☆

Do you feel sleepy on rainy days? If the answer is yes, you are not alone. Many people feel the same way. But do you know why?

One of the reasons is that there is little sunshine on rainy days. Our brain (大脑) will have less serotonin (血清素) and more melatonin (褪黑素) if there is not enough sunlight. Serotonin helps us feel good and energetic, while melatonin makes us feel sleepy. As a result, we may want to go to sleep and even feel a bit sad when it is rainy.

The humidity (湿度) of a rainy day is another reason. As it rains, the air becomes wetter than usual. It's more difficult for our sweat (汗水) to dry in weather like this, so our body has to work harder to stay cool. This can make us feel tired.

Moreover (此外), the sound of rain, a type of pink noise, will make us feel sleepy too. Like white noise, pink noise includes all the sound frequencies (频率) humans can hear, but it has fewer high frequencies (频率) than white noise. This makes pink noise sound softer. Studies show that people's brain waves (脑电波) will become fewer when they hear pink noise, so they will fall asleep more easily.

Now you can understand why you are sleepy on rainy days. Then what can you do if you don't like that feeling? Well, you can get as much natural light as possible and keep yourself busy. Also, you can use a machine to make your room dry. In this way, you won't feel sleepy any more.

( ) 1. (新考法 · 开篇方式题) How does the writer start the passage?

( ) 2. What does the underlined word "energetic" in Paragraph 2 probably mean?

( ) 3. From the fourth paragraph, we can learn _______.

( ) 4. What is the passage mainly about?

☑ 参考答案与解析

1. 【A】By asking a question.
解析：文章开篇第一句"Do you feel sleepy on rainy days?"（你在雨天会感到困倦吗？）是一个疑问句，作者通过提问来引出话题。这是典型的"设问开篇"方式。故选 A。
2. 【B】Active.
解析：根据第二段上下文，serotonin 帮助我们"feel good and energetic"，而 melatonin 让我们"feel sleepy"（感到困倦）。energetic 与 sleepy 形成对比，应表示"精力充沛的、活跃的"意思。B项 Active（活跃的）最接近。A项 Cold（冷的）和C项 Tired（疲倦的）均不符合语境。故选 B。
3. 【C】the softer the noise sounds, the more easily people will feel sleepy
解析：第四段指出粉噪音（pink noise）比白噪音有更少的高频声音，"This makes pink noise sound softer"（这使得粉噪音听起来更柔和），然后"Studies show that people's brain waves will become fewer when they hear pink noise, so they will fall asleep more easily"（研究表明听到粉噪音时人们脑电波减少，更容易入睡）。因此可以推断：声音越柔和，人们越容易感到困倦。故选 C。A项说反了（白噪音高频更多）；B项说反了（粉噪音使脑电波更少而非更多）。
4. 【C】The reasons why people are sleepy in rainy weather.
解析：文章第一段提出问题"雨天为什么会困"，然后第二段讲原因一（阳光少导致血清素少、褪黑素多），第三段讲原因二（湿度大导致身体散热困难），第四段讲原因三（雨声是粉噪音让人困），最后一段给出建议。全文围绕"雨天困倦的原因"展开。故选 C。A项只涉及第四段部分内容；B项"人们需要足够睡眠的原因"偏离主题。
